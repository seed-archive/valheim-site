# The questions each biome guide answers (draft for the owner, 2026-11-19)

**From 2026-11-21 these are the Speedrun page's questions only** (the owner approved the rewrite,
`guide-writing.md`): they were written for the speedrun, and the biome chapters built on them told the usual player
a runner's story. The chapters are written anew from the usual player's question, "how do I explore this biome?";
this list stays as the source of the runner's pages.

The questions below were written for the speedrun (2026-11-19). Since 2026-11-20 the guides tell the usual game as
the baseline and give each way of playing its own section where it needs one (`.claude/rules/site-text.md` → The
baseline and the ways of playing); the questions stay the speedrun's part. As written then: the site answers one
question: **how do I reach the end of the game fastest?** A biome
guide answers it for its biome: **how do I get through this biome fastest?** That means getting what the biome's boss
asks for, making what the next biome needs, and killing the boss. The questions below are that question cut into the
steps a run takes, in their order. A row the guide holds today that answers none of them leaves the page.

A question marked (?) is one I am not sure belongs; the owner decides.

## The start island (before the Meadows)

The main question (the owner, 2026-11-19): **how much time does my start island save me on the way to iron, and
better still to silver?** Copper is most likely on the island already. Every biome on the island is a voyage not
sailed, there and above all back with the load: iron from a Swamp on the island is carried on foot to the copper
base, and a boss on the island is killed without a boat.

1. Which biomes are on my start island?
2. Which bosses' altars are on it, and so how much sailing does that spare me, to iron and to silver?
3. How far can I go without a boat: can I set the Black Forest's base on it, the Swamp's, the Mountain's?
4. Where do I go first? **Scouting.** Run until you know where the iron and the silver will come from: Bonemass
   and Moder. On the way pick up the surtling cores for a portal, mark the copper veins, and rob everything that can
   be robbed, so there are coins for the trader.

## Meadows: Eikthyr

Checked by the owner, 2026-11-19. Eikthyr is killed for speed: his power, his trophy hung at the start, makes running
cost less stamina, so the scouting run reaches Bonemass and Moder sooner, closes the first four biomes sooner, and
learns sooner whether the seed is worth running at all, or whether to drop the run because nothing is near.

1. Where is Eikthyr's altar? Asked for speed: his power shortens the scouting run.
2. How do I kill two deer for his summon? Deer are met on the way anyway; what matters is a throwing weapon, a spear
   or a bow, which kills a deer far more surely than chasing it with a club.
3. How do I kill Eikthyr? Light with a spear or a bow, and on a speedrun he is set on fire with campfires.
4. What do I make from his antler? The antler pickaxe, almost always. (The Stagbreaker, the club that hits all
   round, helps only against the Draugr of the Sunken Crypts and is often skipped; it is made from deer trophies and
   wood, not the antler.)
5. Where are the beehives? Not searched for on purpose: they come out of the Draugr villages and houses robbed on the
   scouting run, along with the axes for fine wood and coins for the trader. Four are usually enough; the site counts
   five.
6. Where is fine wood? Near the troll cave's base, so the troll is led there to fell it (the Black Forest's
   question).
7. Is Hildir worth looking for? No one looks for her: traders are seen from far off and found on the way, while the
   run follows the Vegvísir to the boss as fast as it can.

## Black Forest: the Elder

Checked by the owner, 2026-11-19. Everything turns round the troll cave: the troll mines the copper, fells the
trees, at best the fine wood too.

1. Where do I set my base? By a troll cave with two copper veins by it on the shore, and tin there, since bronze is
   copper and tin. What matters is a real troll spawning by the cave; whether one spawns inside does not.
2. How do I get copper and tin fast? The troll mines the copper. Tin you most likely dig yourself. (By the game's
   data a troll's hit, pickaxe 40 at tier 2, breaks a tin rock, tier 0 and 30 health, in one blow; to be checked.)
3. Where are the Burial Chambers? Only in the Black Forest. Found by running round the base, robbing on the way the
   stone towers, the bear's den and whatever can be robbed, carrying it home, and picking berries and the rest for
   food.
4. How do I make bronze? The real question is the surtling cores, from the Burial Chambers: 14 of them, 5 for the
   charcoal kiln (wood into coal), 5 for the smelter (copper and tin ore into bars), 2 and 2 for a pair of portals.
   Bronze is then made from the bars by its recipe.
5. Where do I get the Elder's three ancient seeds? They come while running round the base: from Greydwarf nests
   when broken, and from Greydwarf brutes (one in three).
6. How do I kill the Elder? Hardly the question; finding him is. His Vegvísir is in the stone towers, so they are
   climbed; once he is found, the run goes straight there and kills him (fire arrows, campfires). He fells wood too,
   so a portal stands by his altar (4 more cores, 2 in, 2 out) and his wood goes home through it in bulk.
7. Where is fine wood for the portal? Felled with the troll, a bronze axe, or the axes from chests that cut it,
   ideally near the altars. A portal takes 20 fine wood, a pair 40.
8. Where is Haldor, and what do I buy from him? Hoped for on the way to the Elder; bought what the coins allow, above
   all Megingjord (950 coins). The run's goods from the Bog Witch, the serving tray and the hamstrings, are cheap.
9. The boat is not a question of its own: the base is a cave by the water, the boat is built on the water facing
   it, at the moment the run sees it must sail to the next boss.

## Swamp: Bonemass

Checked by the owner, 2026-11-19. The Swamp is passed for iron, and for the wishbone that finds silver.

1. How do I reach the Swamp? Most likely by boat. A table: how often the Swamp is on the start island, so the player
   sees he will most likely sail.
2. Where do I look for the Sunken Crypts? Not in a narrow Swamp along the shore, which seldom holds one, but in a big
   Swamp, and a big Swamp is hard to find.
3. How do I get the iron? Into the first room at least, dig iron with the antler pickaxe anyway, and hope for a chest
   with iron in it. Twenty iron and the Vegvísir to Bonemass are all a run needs; with luck the crypts end there, without
   it the run digs and looks for the Vegvísir and gets its iron meanwhile.
4. Where do I smelt it? Right by the crypt: a new smelter and a forge on the Swamp, best with a Surtling fire hole there
   for both fuel and cores. The iron is forged on the spot, most likely into a mace for Bonemass. The bad case: a cart
   loaded with iron to the shore, the iron moved onto the boat, sailed to the first base and smelted there.
5. How do I get the Abominations' roots for the root harness? Two Abominations make it, and it saves the player from
   the Deathsquito on the Plains, so they are worth finding; speedrunners skip it and outrun the Deathsquito, which
   is hard.
6. How do I find Bonemass? (Killing him is not the question: an iron mace, or the speedrunner's way, a shelf built
   and Bonemass hit from under it.)
7. The wishbone needs no question: it finds silver, buried, beeping like a metal detector when worn, and iron in the
   same Swamp if short. A run may skip Bonemass and go straight to the Mountain, hoping Moder's shots dig the silver
   out; a poor hope.
8. Where is the Bog Witch? Often by Bonemass on a big Swamp. Not looked for, except for the squirrel hamstrings of
   the Ratatosk mead.

## Mountain: Moder

Checked by the owner, 2026-11-19. The Mountain is climbed only for Moder's death and the silver: find her, dig the
silver, take it home, and leave.

1. How do I stand the cold? A cape or the frost resistance mead: where do I get each? (The wolf cape takes silver, so
   the first climb is made on the mead.)
2. How do I find silver? With the wishbone.
3. Are there enough drake nests by Moder's altar? Not where they are: they stand round her altar, are seen well and
   found by chance; but there may be too few, and going to another mountain for eggs is bad.
4. How do I get the silver home to the copper base? A cart down the mountain overturns and may break, and may have
   to be moved onto a boat, so a smelter is usually built right at Moder's altar, what is needed is smelted there,
   and the run leaves the mountain. A Mountain close to the copper base is good.
(How to kill Moder is not the question: a bow, or anything. What her tears make is not the Mountain's question.)

## Plains: Yagluth

Checked by the owner, 2026-11-19.

1. How do I get past the Deathsquitos? The root harness, so this question is the Swamp's as much (its Abominations).
2. How do I find Yagluth?
3. How do I find five totems, best right by Yagluth? A big question of the Plains, though not the main one.
4. How do I get into a village: fight, or slip in?
5. How do I get the barley and flax? They are in the villages: kill many Fulings, or steal the crop and run, or grow
   it afterwards. Whether to set a farm is the question, since growing takes more (the cultivator).
(Black metal is not a question: it drops from the Fulings as loot. How to kill Yagluth is not either: the weapon and
whether a fire resistance mead is needed depend on the player's level; the point is finding him.)

## Mistlands: the Queen

Checked by the owner, 2026-11-19. The Mistlands are easy to find and hard to move through: the mist hides
everything, and the cliffs are steep, so the run climbs up and jumps down all the time.

1. How do I make the feather cape and the grappling hook, and where do I find what they take? Both are needed, here
   and after. (A table: what each takes and how easy it is to gather.)
2. How do I see in the mist? The Wisplight: wisp lures set up, the wisps caught, the light made.
3. Is the iron trinket worth making here? It restores health after a hit (the owner's memory; its effect is to be
   read from the game).
4. How do I find the Infested Mines, best by the Queen? The mist hides them, which is why speedrunners may use the
   seed printing command.
5. How many mines do I clear for the Sealbreaker? A table.
6. Where do I get the needle (the Dvergr extractor) for the sap extractor, and how do I take it? The Dvergr guard it
   and kill in one blow at this stage, so it is stolen: where it lies, how to steal it, whether to lead a Gjall onto
   the Dvergr's spawn, or another way.
7. Where do I get eitr? The Yggdrasil root found, a base on it with the workbench, the sap extractor and a portal,
   then back later for the sap; best outside the deep mist, at the edge with the Plains or wherever luck gives.
8. What does the Queen's fight take? Stamina meads and bombs for her swarm of minions.
(Getting into a mine is no question: there is fighting either way. The black cores are no question: they are in the
dungeons. The run usually carries 2 iron for a stonecutter, to break something down into black marble.)

## Ashlands: Fader

Checked by the owner, 2026-11-19. The run runs, with a crowd of mobs behind it: the Ashlands spawn them in great
numbers.

1. On what do I cross the sea? Its water burns and can break the ship, so a sturdier ship is needed.
2. How do I find a Charred Fortress, dodging the mobs?
3. How do I get in? The speedrunners' way: a ladder over the wall onto the tower, down through the hole in its roof
   to the bell fragments; then the tower dug under from four sides until a chest falls out on one of them, with more
   loot, while the mobs falling in are killed. Or a battering ram, the outer wall first, then the tower.
4. How do I get out again?
5. How many fortresses do I need for three bells: three or four? A table.
6. How do I find Fader, fast, with the mobs behind me?
(Not questions: killing no one, which does not work; taking the fragments, which are simply there; the Morgen holes,
since the Morgens run about in numbers and a couple die on the way anyway. How to kill Fader is secondary: the weapon,
the buff, a pit dug under him, a wall round his arena against the other mobs; the point is finding him.)

## Deep North: Kall

Checked by the owner, 2026-11-19. The previous biome ends far in the south, and the run must get far north fast:
it portals to the copper base, somewhere near the map's middle, and sails from there.

1. What do I bring from the earlier biomes? The feather cape, the grappling hook, the strongest weapon found, some
   armour.
2. How many meads, and which, do I bring for Kall? This is what the hives at the very start were for: a stock of mead
   for the last fight.
3. How do I find the three Mörkhalla before Kall? A Mörkhalla is a tower that goes down, not up: the run jumps to its
   bottom and looks there for the moulds (for the weapon that kills Kall, perhaps armour) and what Kall's summon
   asks.
4. How do I find the Winding Tunnels, with the key?
5. Are these four places close to Kall, and is Kall close to the map's vertical axis? A table. (The same for Fader:
   how close to the vertical axis, and how close his Charred Fortresses.)
(How to kill Kall is not the question: with the weapon.)
