# Forge of Potential

**Can you upgrade gear past its last level?** Yes, at the Forge of Potential: one stands in the Mountain of every world, and with an idol of the item's tier it raises a weapon or an armour piece above the level its own station stops at, {{success}}% of the time; the rest of the time the item shatters.

## Where it stands

There is one Forge of Potential a world, an ancient forge on a mountain. The [seed map](/tools/map) marks it among the places, so a run knows before setting out how far the climb is.

## The idols

Every upgrade at the Forge takes, besides the usual materials of that level, one idol of the item's tier: a Battle idol for a weapon, a Protection idol for armour. The tier follows the biome the item belongs to, from the Wooden idols of the Meadows to the Bloodgold idols of the Deep North. Idols lie in chests, in the places of their biome: the Meadows' abandoned houses, the Black Forest's ruins and the Swamp's huts, the Mountain's caves and cabins, the Plains' huts, the towers of the Mistlands, the Ashlands' fortresses and ruins, and the Deep North's shipwrecks and the halls of Mörkhalla. [Caves and dungeons](/catalog/dungeons) lists what each place holds.

## The odds

An upgrade at the Forge either works or breaks the item; there is no middle outcome.

- **{{success}}%**: the item rises a level, past its usual last one if you go on.
- **{{shatter}}%**: the item shatters, and about {{returned}}% of the materials it took come back.

So every try past the usual last level stakes the whole item. Upgrade a spare, or an item you can make again from what you have, and keep the one you rely on.

## Not luck, a fixed answer

The roll is not fresh each time. The game draws it from your character's name, the item and the level you try for, and moves on to the next draw after each try. For a given character the outcome of the next upgrade of an item is already set before you press the button; another character, with another name, gets other answers for the same item.

## Which gear to take there

Take the gear you will keep: the [weapons](/catalog/weapons) and [armour](/catalog/armour) of your last biome, at the level their station allows, and the idols of their tier. Each level past the usual one asks for more of the item's materials than the level before, and the [craft tree](/tools/craft-tree) totals them.
