# Reverse run

**How to play Valheim backwards?** Sail for the late biomes with next to nothing, live through the cold and the first fights, and beat the bosses in the opposite order, getting round each gate the normal order would have opened for you.

## What players mean by it

A reverse run turns the game round: the bosses from the last to the first, or straight to the [Deep North](/guides/deep-north) and the walk home to the [Meadows](/guides/meadows). Any boss can be summoned in any order once its offering is in hand, so nothing forbids it; what makes it hard is that each boss normally hands you the tool for the next. The first written run of all the bosses backwards took tens of hours, most of them spent on one boss whose summon hid behind a key it did not have yet. The route below is learned from the players named in the [credits](/credits).

## The gates on the way back

Every boss of the usual order opens something. Played backwards, each gate has to be got round:

| Gate | What opens it | The way round |
|---|---|---|
| A pickaxe | Eikthyr's hard antler | a troll's blows mine copper and tin for you, silver too |
| The Sunken Crypts | the Elder's key | Bonemass's bones from the Swamp's rare chests, slowly |
| Finding silver | Bonemass's wishbone | dig the mountains blind |
| The artisan table | Moder's tears | without it, no later stations |
| The Queen's door | the Sealbreaker, from the Infested Mines and black metal | the Mistlands first, in full |
| The Ashlands | the Drakkar: the boiling sea burns other boats | build it before going |
| Fader's bells | the Black Forge, from the Mistlands' black cores | the Mistlands before the Ashlands |
| The Deep North's embers | Fader's ribs, for the Eternal Pyre | Fader before going deep |

The straight fight and the trick for every boss are in [How to kill each boss](/guides/bosses).

## Staying alive with nothing

**The cold.** Every weather of the Deep North and the Mountain freezes you, day and night, and so do caves: freezing hurts and stops your health coming back. A fire or a roof alone softens it to cold; both together lift it, and so does any frost resistance: a [frost resistance mead](/catalog/meads), or Moder's power from her [trophy](/catalog/trophies), which a reverse run gets late.

**The first kills.** Start with what does not fight back, and keep a shelter you can repair: a fire under a roof is both warmth and rest. [Comfort](/guides/comfort) says which pieces make that rest last.

**Metal stays where it was mined.** Ore and bars do not pass an ordinary portal, so a reverse run smelts where it mines, until the Ashlands' stone portal carries metal.

## The seed for it

A reverse run wants the late biomes near the start and land between them. The [seed map](/tools/map) shows how far each biome lies; [How good is my seed?](/tools/how-good-is-my-seed) scores the usual order.

The usual order, as fast as it goes, is the [speedrun](/guides/speedrun).
