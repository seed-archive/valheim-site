# Mistlands

**How do I get through the Mistlands fast?** Make the feather cape and the grappling hook for the cliffs, light the mist with the Wisplight, clear the Infested Mines by the Queen for the Sealbreaker, steal a Dvergr extractor for the sap, and set an eitr base on a Yggdrasil root.

The Mistlands are easy to find and hard to cross: the mist hides everything, and the cliffs are steep, so you climb up and jump down all the time. Carry {{stonecutterIron}} iron, for a stonecutter that breaks Dvergr pieces down into black marble.

## How do I make the feather cape and the grappling hook?

Both are needed here and after: the cape takes away the fall, the hook pulls you up a cliff.

## How do I see in the mist?

With the Wisplight: set wisp lures with Yagluth's Torn Spirit, catch the wisps, and bind one with silver at the workbench. The iron trinket is worth making here too: it gives health back after a hit.

## How do I take a Dvergr extractor?

The sap extractor is built with one, and the Dvergr who guard it kill in one blow at this stage, so it is stolen: it lies in a long Dvergr crate in their guard towers, lighthouses, excavations and harbours. The crate has to break without your own blow: anything you hit, throw or shoot at it counts as yours, and the Dvergr turn on you. Two things break it and are not yours. Refined eitr dropped on the ground beside it gives off sparks of lightning that crack it, and it counts as no one's; drop a handful and wait. Or build a cart and ram the crate with it until it breaks. A creature that breaks it draws the Dvergr onto itself, so leading a Gjall or a Seeker into the crate works too.

## Where do I get eitr?

Find a Yggdrasil root, and set a base on it: the workbench, the sap extractor and a portal. Come back later for the sap. Best outside the deep mist, at the edge with the Plains, or wherever luck gives.

## What does the Queen's fight take?

Stamina meads ({{staminaMeadHoney}} honey a base) and bombs for her swarm of minions: Bile Bombs from the Gjall's bilebags. The rest is in [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#the-queen).

> **Speedrunners** may print the seed to find the Infested Mines through the mist, steal the extractor with refined eitr dropped by its crate, or a cart rammed into it, rather than fight the Dvergr, and set the eitr base on the nearest root.

> **With no map**, the mist hides Yggdrasil too, and the Queen is the hardest boss to find: players lay lines of wisp torches, doubled for the way out and single for a dead end.

> **With no portals**, set the base on the edge with the Plains, for the crops, and move it to the southern edge before the Ashlands.

> **On a reverse run** the black metal comes from the Deep North's wardrobes, not the Plains: enough for eitr, and the feather cape and the hook with it.

## Can you skip it?

Not if the run goes on: the Black Forge forges Fader's bells, and the Drakkar, the one boat that survives the Ashlands' sea, needs Yggdrasil wood and the Queen's ceramic plates.
