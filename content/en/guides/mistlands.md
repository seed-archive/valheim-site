# Mistlands

**What do the Mistlands give?** Black marble, Yggdrasil wood, eitr and black cores for the Black Forge and the Galdr Table, and the Queen, whose carapace the Drakkar needs for the Ashlands.

## Getting there

The Mistlands are cliffs and spires under a thick mist. Bring the Wisplight from Yagluth's wisps: its bound wisp guides you through the thickest mist. Bring black metal as well, since every Mistlands station but the Black Forge asks for it. The [seed map](/tools/map) shows the Mistlands, their Infested Mines and the Queen's door.

## Marble, wood and cores

Black marble and Yggdrasil wood stand in the open, and both are beyond the iron tools. A Seeker Soldier's blows break both, so a run lures one beside them instead of making the tools; the black metal pickaxe takes Yggdrasil wood itself. The Infested Mines hold the black cores and the Sealbreaker fragments: see [Caves and dungeons](/catalog/dungeons).

Soft tissue, broken out of the Dvergr excavations and towers, becomes eitr at the eitr refinery, which burns sap. Seekers drop carapace and hares scale hide; the Hook lies in chests of the Dvergr ruins and the Infested Mines. Where each comes from is in [Materials](/catalog/materials).

## What to make

The Black Forge is black marble, Yggdrasil wood and a black core; the Galdr Table adds black metal and eitr. The Carapace set at the Black Forge is the armour to make, and Krom, the Demolisher and the Arbalest are the weapons for the Queen: the [weapons](/catalog/weapons), [bows](/catalog/bows) and [armour](/catalog/armour) pages compare them, and the [craft tree](/tools/craft-tree) totals the marble and the eitr.

Make the Grappling Hook at the Black Forge and the Feather Cape at the Galdr Table, and cook Misthare Supreme: see [Food and feasts](/catalog/food).

## The Queen

Her door is sealed, and the Sealbreaker breaks it: the Galdr Table forges it from {{sealbreakerFragments}} Sealbreaker fragments. She resists pierce and ignores spirit; the straight fight is Krom or the Demolisher. Players who would rather not trade blows throw Bile Bombs, made from the Gjall's bilebags, and keep to the entrance, where nothing spawns. Both ways, and her numbers, are in [How to kill each boss](/guides/bosses#the-queen).

She drops the Majestic Carapace, which builds the Artisan Press beside the artisan table; only the raised table turns black marble into ceramic plates, and the Drakkar is built with them. Her trophy on its stone at the start makes sneaking free, speeds up eitr and takes the edge off poison: see [Trophies](/catalog/trophies).

## Can you skip it?

Not if the run goes on. The Black Forge forges Fader's bells, and the Drakkar, the one boat that survives the Ashlands' sea, needs both Yggdrasil wood and the Queen's ceramic plates. Skipping the Mistlands ends the run before the Ashlands.
