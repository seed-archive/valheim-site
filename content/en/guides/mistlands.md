# Mistlands

**What do I need from the Mistlands?** Clear the Infested Mines by the Queen for the Sealbreaker and the black cores, and steal a Dvergr extractor for the sap that becomes eitr.

Yagluth's Torn Spirit in your pack, you sail for the mist. The Mistlands are easy to find and hard to cross: the mist hides everything, and the cliffs are steep, so you climb up and jump down all day. Pack {{stonecutterIron}} iron before you sail: a stonecutter set on the spot breaks the Dvergr's pieces down into black marble.

## How far are the Mistlands from home?

Far enough that everything you take, the black marble and the Yggdrasil wood above all, goes home by boat: the mist brushes only the far edge of a lucky start island, and never the Queen's own ground. Her gate as a rule lies {{queenM}} m from the start.

## Does a cleared mine stay clear?

Not for long. A mine's Seekers crawl back {{respawnSeekerMin}} minutes of world time after the last one was seen, unless a piece of your base covers the spot they rose from: a standing torch rings {{baseRadiusBed}} m, a workbench {{baseRadiusWorkbench}} m ([Boss arenas](/guides/boss-arenas#which-pieces-make-the-ground-a-base) gives every piece). Out in the mist they rise anywhere outside a base. Inside a mine a harder world makes them hit harder, but no faster and no larger, so a mine is the calmer place to fight them, and their carapace is the Mistlands' armour:

## Where do I get black marble and black cores for the stations?

Your home of this age wants both: black marble is the strongest building stone until the Ashlands, and the Black Forge is built of it ([Crafting stations](/catalog/crafting-stations)). It comes from the giants' remains, which the black metal pickaxe from the [Plains](/guides/plains) breaks, and from the Dvergr pieces your stonecutter breaks down. The first remains as a rule lie {{queenMarbleM}} m from the Queen's gate ({{queenMarbleMLow}}–{{queenMarbleMHigh}} m on the middle half of seeds).

> **Speedrunners** let a Seeker Soldier do the mining: lead it so its ram and slam land on a giant's ribs, and it breaks them for you, so the remains become a quarry without a swing of your own.

The black cores sit in the Infested Mines. The Black Forge takes {{keyForgeBlackCores}}, the Galdr Table {{galdrBlackCores}}, the eitr refinery {{refineryBlackCores}}: {{blackCoresAll}} for all three.

## How do I take a Dvergr extractor?

You steal it. The sap extractor is built with one, and the Dvergr who guard it kill in one blow at this stage. It lies in a long Dvergr crate in their guard towers, lighthouses, excavations and harbours, and the crate has to break without your blow: anything you hit, throw or shoot at it counts as yours, and the Dvergr turn on you. Three things break it and are not yours. Build a cart and ram the crate until it gives. Or lead a creature into it: a creature that breaks it draws the Dvergr onto itself, so a Gjall or a Seeker led into the crate does the job. The third waits for your second extractor, once the first gives sap and the refinery turns it into refined eitr: drop a handful beside the crate and wait, and the sparks of lightning crack it, counting as no one's.

## Where do I set the eitr base?

On a Yggdrasil root, with the workbench, the sap extractor and a portal, then come back later for the sap. Best outside the deep mist, where the Mistlands meet the [Plains](/guides/plains#can-you-skip-the-plains): the barley grows next door and the base can stay for good. Otherwise, wherever a root gives you the chance. Build it to last: from Yagluth's death until the Queen's, Gjall and Seekers raid the bases you stand in.

## Coming from the north, what must I build here?

The Black Forge, even on a [reverse run](/guides/reverse-run) that leaves the Queen for last: the Intricate Key that opens the Mörkhalla is cast on it at level {{keyForgeLevel}}, and the forge is built only of what the Mistlands give. Any three extensions set beside it raise it to that level. The usual game takes the Cooler, the Vice and the Metal Cutter from the Ashlands; a run from the north swaps the Cutter's flametal for the Smith's Aprons' Bloodgold, which needs Fader's embers anyway. The Vice's mechanical springs are pressed at the artisan table from iron and refined eitr, so even the key waits on this biome's sap.

## How do I make the feather cape and the grappling hook?

The cape takes away the fall, so you jump off the cliffs instead of climbing down; the hook pulls you up them. The hook goes north with you, for a Mörkhalla's raised door. The cape ends the [Deep North](/guides/deep-north)'s freeze too, but it doubles the fire you take, so Kall's fight is the reason to leave it home.

## How do I see in the mist?

With the Wisplight: set wisp lures with Yagluth's Torn Spirit from the [Plains](/guides/plains), catch the wisps, and bind one with silver from the [Mountain](/guides/mountain) at the workbench.

## How do I find my way in the mist with no map?

The Queen is the hardest boss to find without a map, and the mist steals the sky and Yggdrasil with it, so you steer by what you lay down and what the Dvergr built. Lay lines of wisp torches, doubled for the way out and single for a dead end, mine the ridges flat into a road, and keep to the ground you have cleared. Come in from the edge with the Plains, where the sky still shows, and if a root stands near it, make it your home.

## What does the Queen's fight take?

Stamina meads, from the [hives](/guides/meadows#how-many-hives-for-brewing-all-game) you keep at home, and bombs for her swarm of minions: Bile Bombs from the Gjall's bilebags. The rest is in [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#the-queen).

## Can you skip the Mistlands?

Not if you mean to go on. The Black Forge, built with the mines' black cores, forges Fader's bells and casts the Intricate Key, and the Drakkar, the one boat that survives the Ashlands' sea, takes {{drakkarYggdrasil}} Yggdrasil wood and {{drakkarPlates}} ceramic plates, pressed on an artisan table that only the Queen's drop raises.

[How good is my seed?](/tools/how-good-is-my-seed) sets your own seed against these numbers, and the [seed map](/tools/map) shows where its mines and roots stand. Next, the burning sea and Fader: the [Ashlands](/guides/ashlands).
