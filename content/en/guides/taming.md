# Taming animals

**How do I tame an animal?** Keep it fed and calm near you for {{tamingMin}} minutes: drop its food where it can reach it, stay out of its sight, and it is yours.

## Which animals can be tamed?

{{tameCount}} of them, one or two a biome, and nothing else: drakes, seals, deer and trolls cannot be tamed without mods. Each eats only its own food, and only the wolf takes anything cooked.

Figure: tames

The wolf is the one that follows you and stays on command. The hen is never found wild: it hatches tame from an egg.

## How does taming work?

The animal must be fed, calm and near you, all three at once. It eats what lies on the ground within reach of it and stays fed for {{fedMin}} minutes, so {{foodsToTame}} of its foods carry it through the whole {{tamingMin}} minutes. Calm means it does not see you, a fire or an enemy as a threat: the moment it is alerted the clock stops, and starts again where it stopped. Walk away and nothing happens either; sleeping through the night only eats the food faster.

The {{meadName}} speeds it: while someone who drank it stands within {{boostM}} m, taming runs {{boostTimes}} times as fast. Its base is brewed at the mead ketill:

With it the {{tamingMin}} minutes become {{boostedMin}}, as long as the one who drank it stays near.

## How do I catch each one?

The point of a pen or a pit is to keep the animal from running or fighting while it calms, with its food at its feet and you out of its sight.

- **Boar.** A wooden pen with a gate: a boar charges you, so let it chase you in, step out and shut the gate. Keep fire and torches away; a wild boar fears fire and will not eat beside it.
- **Wolf.** A pit with a roof, or a stone pen: starred wolves break wood. They come only at night and leave by day, unless taming has already started.
- **Lox.** A wooden pen will not hold one. Scatter its food where a herd stands and keep out of sight; it will not eat under a low roof.
- **Asksvin.** A shallow pit or walls on level ground, and the [Abyssal Harpoon](/catalog/weapons#spearchitin) to drag it in. A very deep pit can keep it from calming at all.
- **Moose.** Stone walls round it, its food thrown in from outside.
- **Hen.** There is nothing to catch: buy an egg from [Haldor](/catalog/traders) once [Yagluth](/catalog/bosses#yagluth) is dead, or take one from the chest of Hildir's [Sealed Tower](/catalog/dungeons#sealed-tower-hildirs-chest), and hatch it.

## How do I hatch an egg?

Drop it on the ground: in a chest or in your pack an egg never hatches. It must lie alone, not in a pile, within the heat of a fire, and a hen's egg also under a roof that covers most of the sky above it; an asksvin's egg needs the fire only. Then it hatches after {{eggMin}} minutes of the world's time, and sleeping through the night counts. Move it, pick it up or let the fire die, and the count starts over. The chick is tame and has as many stars as its egg.

## What if I stop feeding it?

It stays tame and it does not starve. A hungry tame only stops healing and stops breeding, and a hungry mount gets its stamina back at half the speed. Feed it again and it is whole once more.

## How do I slaughter a tame?

With the [Butcher Knife](/catalog/weapons#knifebutcher), made at the forge: it hurts nothing but tamed animals, and nothing else of yours will hurt them except a blow you strike on purpose. It is how you take meat and hide from your own herd without breaking it.

## How do they breed?

Two tamed animals of a kind, both fed and calm and close together, fall in love; after a few checks one of them is pregnant and soon gives birth. Each kind stops once enough of it stands near. The young are born tame and grow up on their own; an egg has to be kept by a fire first.

Figure: breeding

A herd stops growing at the number under Stops at, counted within the distance beside it: lead the grown ones farther off, and the pair breeds on. The lox is the slow one, in pregnancy and in growing up alike; the rest keep one pace.

## Can I ride it?

Three of them take a saddle, the lox, the asksvin and the moose; each saddle in the first table links its recipe.

Riding is steering with the camera: forward walks, run gallops, back stops. A gallop and a swim use the saddle's stamina, and the Ride skill makes both last longer and the mount run faster. Your weight and your armour do not slow it. An asksvin carries you over lava unhurt.

Figure: mounts

The saddles are alike: the same stamina and the same drain, so a gallop lasts as long on any of the three. What differs is the animal: the moose is the fastest by far, and the asksvin is the one that walks on lava.

You cannot fight from the saddle: an attack, a jump or a dodge throws you off, so no bow and no blade while mounted. The mount fights for itself, though, and a galloping lox tramples what stands in its way. No animal follows you through a portal.

## Which ones are worth it?

The hen if you shoot: arrows eat feathers, and a coop gives them without a hunt. If you fight with a blade or a staff, you need few feathers and can do without her. A pack of starred wolves is the strongest guard a base can have, and an asksvin the fastest road across the Ashlands. Boars pay only early, for leather and meat; the lox and the moose are for riding and for meat by the stack.
