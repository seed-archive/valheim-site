# Speedrun

**How to finish Valheim fast?** Pick a seed with the altars close together, make only what the next boss needs, let creatures cut and mine for you, and plan every bar of metal before you swing a pickaxe.

## What counts as a run

The community times Valheim as All Bosses: Classic ends with Yagluth, the later categories add the Queen, Fader and Kall Fimbulbringer. Each comes on a random seed or a set one, and glitchless allows playing a boss's mind but not clipping through walls. The categories, their rules and today's times are on the [Valheim leaderboards of speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/valheim). The player whose runs taught this guide is in the [credits](/credits).

## Choosing the seed

A fast seed has Eikthyr's altar a short walk from the start, a troll cave with copper by its door and tin on the shore below it, and the Elder close to both. [How good is my seed?](/tools/how-good-is-my-seed) scores a seed on exactly these distances, and the [seed map](/tools/map) shows where everything stands, so the route is planned before the first step.

## The route, boss by boss

**Eikthyr.** Two kills of deer give the {{deerTrophies}} trophies his altar takes; his hard antler makes the only pickaxe before bronze. The [Meadows](/guides/meadows) chapter has the distances.

**The Elder.** Copper and tin become bronze at a troll cave; {{ancientSeeds}} ancient seeds from Greydwarf nests summon him. His key opens the Sunken Crypts. The [Black Forest](/guides/black-forest) chapter has the cave and the tin.

**Bonemass.** {{witheredBones}} withered bones from the Sunken Crypts, iron from their muck piles, and a mace: he is weak to blunt. See the [Swamp](/guides/swamp).

**Moder.** {{dragonEggs}} dragon eggs from drake nests, silver found with Bonemass's wishbone, and a bow: she fights in the air. See the [Mountain](/guides/mountain).

**Yagluth.** {{goblinTotems}} Fuling totems from the Plains' villages. See the [Plains](/guides/plains).

**The Queen, Fader and Kall Fimbulbringer.** The later categories go on through the [Mistlands](/guides/mistlands), the [Ashlands](/guides/ashlands) and the [Deep North](/guides/deep-north). The straight fight and the trick for every boss are in [How to kill each boss](/guides/bosses).

## Saving time

**Let creatures do the work.** A troll's blows reach a bronze tool's tier and more: lured past birches and oaks it fells fine wood, and on copper, tin and even silver it mines. The Elder's blows fell the plain trees round his altar. Later a Seeker Soldier breaks black marble and Yggdrasil wood. The rules allow it.

**Skip what changes nothing.** The bronze pickaxe mines nothing the antler one does not; the Early Axes cut fine wood without bronze, from two axe heads found in the Meadows' abandoned houses.

**Eat to the clock.** Three food slots last as long as the run and no longer, so a run cooks only what it will eat: [Food and feasts](/catalog/food) gives each dish's duration.

## The plan before the pickaxe

The [craft tree](/tools/craft-tree) is the run on one page: switch on what you will make, and it lists every station level it needs and the raw materials it all takes, biome by biome. Gather to that list and nothing more.

Playing it the other way round, the late biomes first, is the [reverse run](/guides/reverse-run).
