# Plains

**What do I need from the Plains?** Yagluth, bread and black metal: find his altar, take the Fuling totems round it, towers first, and bring home barley, flax and the next age's metal.

## How do I get past the Deathsquitos?

Off the Mountain and onto the open gold of the Plains, the first thing that finds you is a whine in the air. Wear the Root Harnesk from the [Swamp](/guides/swamp)'s Abominations and keep a bow in hand. A Deathsquito stings for heavy pierce damage, which the harness halves, but it has almost no health: shoot it as it dives and the fight is over before the sting lands.

## Where is Yagluth's altar?

His Vegvísir waits in the Plains' standing stones and stone towers, so no tower you pass goes unchecked; each one also guards a Fuling totem, and you will need those soon. Expect a long road: he rarely stands near home.

## How do I find the Fuling totems?

His altar takes {{yagluthTotemsNeed}} Fuling totems, one on each holder, and the best are right round it. Every Fuling outpost keeps one at the foot of its tower, always; a village hides one only in its bonfire rooms, at a coin toss each, so one village gives two and the next none. Walk outward from the altar, towers first, until the last holder is filled.

## Can a cleared village be my camp?

Yes, and it is the safe road to the totems if you mean to stay a while. A village's Shamans and its first Fulings rise from spawners that fire once: clear it by day, set down a workbench and a campfire, and while they stand nothing new rises inside their reach, neither from the village nor from the open Plains. Fulings still walk in from outside, so the camp is a place to rest and mend, not a wall. Two pieces stay under the {{raidBasePieces}} that call most raids, so the camp draws none either. From there, take the next village.

## How much black metal do I need?

Enough to wear it, and the kit is the big bill. The Fulings drop it as scrap, a cleared village is a heap of it, and only the blast furnace smelts it, built with Moder's tears from the [Mountain](/guides/mountain). Like every ore, it never passes the wooden portal: it sails home, or the furnace comes to it. What the age is worth, raised to the top:

## How do I get the barley and flax?

They grow in the villages and nowhere else in the world: steal the crop and run, or clear the village and take it all. Every plant you keep is the seed of a farm, which wants the cultivator ([Farming](/guides/farming)); bread and every late food grow from it, so the farm is the Plains' longest gift.

## What does Yagluth's fight take?

The weapon and a fire resistance mead depend on your level ([How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#yagluth)). He hits with fire and lightning, and the Root Harnesk takes fire harder, so change out of it before the summon. His death ends the Fulings' raids and opens the Mistlands' own; his power strengthens every blow for a while and raises your farming; his Torn Spirit builds the wisp lure that lights the Mistlands.

## Can you skip the Plains?

Only by giving up what follows: black metal goes into the Galdr Table and the Queen's Sealbreaker, the crops into the late food, and without the Torn Spirit you walk the Mistlands blind in the mist.
