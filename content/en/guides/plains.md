# Plains

**How do I explore the Plains?** Sail along their shore to Yagluth's altar, build a walled base on level ground by the sea, raid the smallest Fuling village for barley, flax and your first black metal, then take the bigger villages and the towers for his totems.

A Plains lies on your home's own land on {{islandPlains}}% of seeds; on the others you come by boat, and the boat is the better way to look for him either way, since Yagluth's altar stands where it shows from the shore. Sail the coast until you see it, and land near it or on the first good level ground by the sea.

Build there what you built in the Meadows: a base on open ground by the water, walled this time, with a portal. It is not a camp to leave. Barley and flax grow on Plains ground and nowhere else, so this base becomes your farm.

## What kills a newcomer on the Plains?

A whine in the air, first. The Deathsquito has almost no health and a heavy sting, all of it pierce, and the Root Harnesk from the Swamp halves it. Never run from one: it follows you onto the beach and out over the water. You can kill it two ways. A shield takes the dive, and the blow after it kills; this costs nothing and asks for nerve. An arrow kills it in the air; this is safe when it lands and wasted when it does not.

The Fulings come in bands, with spears, and by night there are more of them. Their villages hold the shamans, who shield the others and throw fire, and the berserkers, each with more health than a troll. The lox graze in herds and leave you alone until you come close; one lox has more health than a troll, and the herd comes together. At the tar pits the Growths spit tar that slows you to a walk.

## How do I get the barley and flax?

From the Fuling villages, and nowhere else in the world. Take the smallest village first: you came in wolf armour, and it is not enough for a large one. Pull the Fulings out a few at a time, kill the shamans before the rest, and meet the berserker alone.

One small village gives you everything the next step needs: barley and flax from its fields, a totem if its bonfire has one, and black metal scrap from every Fuling that falls. The blast furnace, built at the artisan table Moder's tears paid for, smelts the scrap, and the first black metal weapons are made.

The first harvest is seed. Plant all of it on the Plains ground by your base and eat none: the second harvest is the one that feeds you. The windmill turns barley into flour for bread, the spinning wheel turns flax into linen thread, and linen with iron makes the padded armour, which takes {{kitBlackMetalIron}} iron at its top: one more trip to the Swamp. Cloudberries cannot be planted, so mark where they grow. As a rule Yagluth's own Plains hold {{yagluthBarley}} barley plants and {{yagluthFlax}} flax in their villages.

## How do I find the Fuling totems?

Yagluth's altar takes {{yagluthTotemsNeed}} of them, one on each of its holders. Every Fuling outpost keeps one at the foot of its tower, always. A village has one only in its bonfire rooms, and each room holds it only half the time, so one village gives two and the next none. Now that black metal is on your back, the big villages are yours too.

## Where is Yagluth's altar?

On the shore you sailed along, and his Vegvísir in the Plains' standing stones and stone towers confirms it. A Fuling village stands within {{campReachMidM}} m of his altar on {{yagluthVillageMid}}% of seeds: clear it before you lay the totems, or it joins the fight.

How to fight him is a choice with a number in it. He takes a quarter of what an arrow should do. The bow still works and keeps you far from his fire and his lightning, at the price of hundreds of arrows. A blade ends it much sooner and stands you in the fire: the crystal battleaxe, the black metal battleaxe and the silver sword do the most. Either way drink the fire resistance wine first, and take the Root Harnesk off, since it burns. Every way to kill him is on [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#yagluth).

His death gives you the Torn Spirit, which builds the wisp fountain, and a wisp caught there with a bar of silver makes the Wisplight. His power adds {{yagluthPowerDamage}}% to every blow, {{yagluthPowerFarming}} levels to your farming and takes {{yagluthPowerLightning}}% off lightning, for {{powerMinutes}} minutes. The Fulings stop raiding your home, and the creatures of the mist start.

You leave the Plains in padded armour with black metal in hand, a farm that bakes bread, and a light that parts the mist. The land it was made for hides everything else it has, its mines, its giants and its queen: the [Mistlands](/guides/mistlands). On the clock the Plains are five totems and a fight, and the [Speedrun](/guides/speedrun#plains) tells it.
