# Plains

**How do I explore the Plains?** Build a walled base by the sea, raid a small Fuling village for barley, flax and black metal, then take the bigger villages and towers for Yagluth's totems.

A Plains lies on your home's own land on {{islandPlains}}% of seeds; on the others you come by boat, and the boat is the better way to look for Yagluth either way, since his altar stands where it shows from the shore. Sail the coast until you see it, and land near it or on the first good level ground by the sea.

Build there what you built in the Meadows: a base on open ground by the water, walled this time, with a portal. It is not a camp to leave. Barley and flax grow on Plains ground and nowhere else, so this base becomes your farm.

## What kills a newcomer on the Plains?

You hear the first one before you see it. The Deathsquito has almost no health and a heavy sting, all of it pierce, and the Root Harnesk you made in the Swamp halves it. Never run from one: it follows you onto the beach and out over the water. You can kill it two ways. A shield takes the dive, and the blow after it kills; this costs nothing and asks for nerve. An arrow that hits kills it before it reaches you; an arrow that misses leaves you holding a bow with the sting on its way.

The Fulings come in bands, with spears, and by night there are more of them. Their villages hold the shamans, who shield the others and throw fire, and the berserkers, each with more health than a troll. The lox graze in herds and leave you alone until you come close; one lox has more health than a troll, and the herd comes together. Fed and left in peace, a lox becomes a mount instead: [Taming animals](/guides/taming) tells how. At the tar pits the Growths spit tar. Whether to clear a pit now is yours to weigh: the Growths are weak to blunt and to fire, and what they guard is tar, which only darkwood building asks for.

## How do I get the barley and flax?

From the Fuling villages, and nowhere else in the world. Take the smallest village first: you came in wolf armour, and it is not enough for a large one. Pull the Fulings out a few at a time, kill the shamans before the rest, and meet the berserker alone.

One small village gives you everything the next step needs: barley and flax from its fields, a totem if its bonfire has one, and black metal scrap from every Fuling that falls. The blast furnace, built at the artisan table Moder's tears paid for, smelts the scrap, and the first black metal weapons are made.

The first harvest is seed. Plant all of it on the Plains ground by your base and eat none: the second harvest is the one that feeds you. The windmill turns barley into flour for bread, the spinning wheel turns flax into linen thread, and linen with iron makes the padded armour, and the age's whole kit takes {{kitBlackMetalIron}} iron at its top: one more trip to the Swamp. Barley and cloudberries also brew the fire resistance barley wine you will want at the end. Bread, lox meat pie and blood pudding are the Plains' food. Cloudberries cannot be planted, so mark where they grow. As a rule Yagluth's own Plains hold {{yagluthBarley}} barley plants and {{yagluthFlax}} flax in their villages.

No ore and no vein: black metal comes only as scrap, from the Fulings you cut down and the chests of their villages and towers.

## How do I find the Fuling totems?

Yagluth's altar takes {{yagluthTotemsNeed}} of them, one on each of its holders. Every Fuling outpost keeps one at the foot of its tower, always. A village has one only in its bonfire rooms, and each room holds it only half the time, so one village gives two and the next none. In padded armour, with black metal in hand, the big villages are within your strength.

## Where is Yagluth's altar?

On the shore you sailed along, and his Vegvísir in the Plains' standing stones and stone towers confirms it. A Fuling village stands within {{campReachMidM}} m of his altar on {{yagluthVillageMid}}% of seeds: clear it before you lay the totems, or it joins the fight.

How to fight him is a choice with a number in it. He takes {{yagluthPierceShare}}% of what an arrow should do. The bow still works and keeps you far from his fire and his lightning, at the price of hundreds of arrows. A blade does its full damage, ends it much sooner, and stands you in the fire: the crystal battleaxe, the black metal battleaxe and the silver sword do the most. Either way drink the fire resistance barley wine first, and take the Root Harnesk off, since it burns. Every way to kill him is on [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#yagluth).

His death gives you the Torn Spirit, which builds the wisp fountain, and a wisp caught there with {{wisplightSilver}} silver makes the Wisplight. His power adds {{yagluthPowerDamage}}% to every blow, {{yagluthPowerFarming}} levels to your farming and takes {{yagluthPowerLightning}}% off lightning, for {{powerMinutes}} minutes. The Fulings stop raiding your home, and the creatures of the mist start.

You leave the Plains in padded armour with black metal in hand, a farm that bakes bread, and the Wisplight. It was made for one land, where the mist hides the mines, the giants and the Queen: the [Mistlands](/guides/mistlands). On the clock the Plains are {{yagluthTotemsNeed}} totems and a fight, and the [Speedrun](/guides/speedrun#plains) tells it.
