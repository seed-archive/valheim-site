# Challenges

**How else can you play through the world?** Against the clock, backwards, without dying, or without a map and portals: each is the same world with one rule more, and each changes what a run must plan for.

## Speedrun

The usual order as fast as it goes: a seed with the altars close, only what the next boss needs, creatures cutting and mining for you. The route, boss by boss, is in the [speedrun](/guides/speedrun) guide.

## Reverse run

The bosses backwards, the late biomes first, and every gate the usual order would have opened got round by hand. The gates and the ways round them are in the [reverse run](/guides/reverse-run) guide; the players who taught it are in the [credits](/credits).

## No death

One life for the whole run: the first death ends it. The world itself does not change, so the plan does: the straight fights give way to the safe ones, a boss is met at a range or from a height, and every danger is met with its resistance on. [How to kill each boss](/guides/bosses) gives each boss the way that keeps you out of its reach, and the [meads](/catalog/meads) list the resistances. The player who taught this run is in the [credits](/credits).

## No map

A setting of the world switches the map off: every place is found by sight and every way back is remembered. The [seed map](/tools/map), studied before the run, is the map you are allowed to remember.

The [Jotun Invasion](/catalog/events#jotun-invasion) is the hardest of these: once you break the Malicious Ice in the Deep North, it stands far out in the world and the map would mark it. With no map the game leaves one clue, a trail of particles pointing to the nearest invasion, so it can be found, slowly.

## No portals

A second setting, apart from the map, switches the portals off: a portal takes you nowhere, so every trip is walked or sailed. Ore and metal never passed a wooden portal anyway, so for metal little changes; what changes is everything else, the food, the wood and the trips home.

## Your own rules

These are the common ones, and there are communities that keep their own lists of challenges, with house rules, records and runs to join. Pick one, or make your own rule and play the world again.
