# Mountain

**How do I explore the Mountain?** Climb by day on a frost mead, dig the first silver the wishbone finds and turn it into a wolf fur cape; then comb the Mountain for the rest of its silver, its drake nests and Moder's altar.

## How do I stand the cold?

Every weather on the Mountain freezes you, by day and by night: your health stops coming back and drains until you are dead. The frost resistance mead you brewed in the Swamp lifts it while it lasts. Leave a few meads in a chest at the mountain's foot before you climb: if you die up there with every one of them on you, you cannot reach your own grave.

Then the creatures. Wolves hunt in packs and outrun you, so never let your stamina run out with one in sight, and climb a rock they cannot. Drakes circle their nests and spit frost; they are weak to fire, and a bow reaches them. A stone golem resists blades and arrows and is very weak to the pickaxe, so you either break it with the tool in your hand or walk away from it, since it is slow; both are sound. At night the Fenrings come out, and the wolves come oftener and starred: climb by day, and be down or under a roof by dusk.

Camp like this: a portal at the mountain's foot, and by the first vein a hut dug into the slope, walled and roofed, with a fire inside. A fire under a roof lifts the freeze even without a mead.

## How do I find the silver?

With Bonemass's wishbone. Silver lies buried on the high, level shelves, never on a slope and never at the Mountain's edge; worn, the wishbone beeps above a vein, and you dig where it beeps. Only the iron pickaxe breaks the vein.

The first silver has one job: the wolf fur cape, of wolf pelts, a little silver and a wolf's trophy. The cape keeps the cold off for good, and from then on the mead stays in the chest. The wolf armour follows.

No metal passes a portal, so the silver is carried down the mountain. Where your home can be reached on foot, it rides a cart all the way; where it cannot, a cart takes it to the boat. Going down, pick the gentlest slope: a loaded cart on a steep one overturns and may break. Silver melts in the smelter you already have.

## What does the whole Mountain hold?

With the cape on you can comb the Mountain at your own pace.

The wolves give the armour: the wolf set and the cape take {{killsWolf}} wolves' pelts, and {{killsWolfTop}} raised to the top. Their meat, with the onions whose seeds lie in the chests of the old cabins, is the Mountain's food. Obsidian lies black on the snow and makes arrows. The frost caves are the Mountain's dungeons: Ulvs, cultists and bats inside, and the Fenris hair of the Fenris set; {{vegvisirCave}}% of the caves hold Moder's Vegvísir, as the tower ruins do.

Silver also makes the weapons of the next lands. The Draugr Fang is the bow for Moder herself; Frostner and the silver sword are for what comes after her. The pieces are on the [weapons](/catalog/weapons) page.

One Mountain of every world holds the [Forge of Potential](/guides/forge-of-potential), which raises a piece past its top: {{forgeChance}}% of tries succeed, and a failed one destroys the piece.

## Where is Moder's altar?

Where her Vegvísir shows it, high on her own Mountain: {{moderAltitudeM}} m above the sea as a rule, and {{altarShoreModer}} m from the shore where your boat waits.

She is weak to fire and untouched by frost, and much of the fight she spends in the air: this is a bow's fight, and which arrow is yours to choose. By the numbers the Draugr Fang with poison arrows does the most to her, then the huntsman bow, then the finewood bow. Fire arrows gain from her weakness to fire, and any bow shoots them. Frost arrows do nothing. She breathes frost, so the cape or the mead stays on. Every way to kill her is on [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#moder).

Her death gives you dragon tears, and the tears build the artisan table, without which there is no blast furnace, no windmill and no spinning wheel: the Plains' metal, bread and linen all wait on her. Her power fills your sail with a tailwind, lets you carry {{moderPowerCarry}} more, makes you {{moderPowerSpeed}}% faster and takes {{moderPowerFrost}}% off the frost you suffer, for {{powerMinutes}} minutes. The drakes stop coming for your home.

You come down from the Mountain in wolf fur that keeps the cold off, with a silver bow, onions for the garden and an artisan table waiting to be built. What the table makes needs black metal, and black metal is carried by the Fulings of the [Plains](/guides/plains). On the clock the Mountain is one climb, and the [Speedrun](/guides/speedrun#mountain) tells it.
