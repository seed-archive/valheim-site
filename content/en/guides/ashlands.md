# Ashlands

**What do the Ashlands give?** Flametal for the Black Forge and its gear, the stone portal that carries metal, and Fader, whose ribs light the embers the Deep North asks for.

## Getting there

The sea round the Ashlands boils, and only the Drakkar survives it: it is built at the workbench with Yggdrasil wood and the Queen's ceramic plates, see [Ships and carts](/catalog/ships). The land itself has lava, so brew a Fire Resistance Barley Wine from the Plains' barley before sailing: see [Meads](/catalog/meads). The [seed map](/tools/map) shows the Ashlands, their fortresses and Fader's altars.

## Flametal, bells and cores

The fortresses hold bell fragments, molten cores, flametal ore in their chests, bloodstone and celestial feathers. Flametal ore is also mined from the lava leviathans, and the blast furnace smelts it. The Charred drop charred bone and are weak to spirit; Asksvins give hide, the Morgen hearts and sinew.

Build the stone portal first: grausten, molten cores and greydwarf eyes at the stonecutter. It is the one portal that takes ore and metal. Where each material comes from is in [Materials](/catalog/materials).

## What to make

The Black Forge rises a level with the Metal Cutter, built of black marble, flametal, ashwood and charred bone. Then flametal, Asksvin hide and charred bone make the Flametal set, and the Morgen heart goes into its breastplate. The Slayer, Nidhögg and the Berserkir Axes are the blades, and a bloodstone, jade or iolite takes each further. The [weapons](/catalog/weapons) and [armour](/catalog/armour) pages compare them, and the [craft tree](/tools/craft-tree) totals the flametal. Cook Piquant Pie: see [Food and feasts](/catalog/food).

## Fader

His altar takes {{bells}} bells, and each bell is forged from {{bellFragments}} bell fragments at the Black Forge. Every Charred Fortress has one altar that breaks into {{altarFragmentsMin}} to {{altarFragmentsMax}} fragments, so {{bellForts}} fortresses are enough in {{bellFortsShare}}% of runs, {{bellFortsMore}} in {{bellFortsMoreShare}}%, and {{bellFortsAll}} always. He is immune to fire and spirit and resists pierce, so the straight fight is a Slayer: the Primal and the Scourging Slayer hit him hardest. Players who would rather not trade blows send trolls from the Trollstav at him and stay out of his reach. Both ways, and his numbers, are in [How to kill each boss](/guides/bosses#fader).

He drops the Kindled Ribs, and the Eternal Pyre is built from stone and the ribs at the stonecutter. The pyre gives embers: they make the Snow Shovel, which clears away the deepest snow, and the Ember Charge. His trophy on its stone at the start takes the edge off fire and staggering: see [Trophies](/catalog/trophies).

## Can you skip it?

At a price the Deep North collects. Without Fader there are no embers, so no Snow Shovel; without flametal there is no Flametal set to wear into the cold; and without the stone portal every bar of the Deep North goes home by boat.
