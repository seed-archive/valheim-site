# Ashlands

**How do I get through the Ashlands fast?** Cross the burning sea in the Drakkar, find three or four Charred Fortresses near Fader with a crowd of mobs behind you, break into their towers for the bell fragments, forge the bells and find Fader.

The Ashlands spawn mobs in great numbers: you run, and they run after you.

## On what do I cross the sea?

The Ashlands' water hurts every boat, the more the longer it sails, so the sturdier Drakkar pays ([Ships and carts](/catalog/ships)); a raft burns at once. On a seed where Fader stands on the shore, even a short sail does. Brew a Fire Resistance Barley Wine from the Plains' barley before sailing ([Meads](/catalog/meads)).

## How do I get in, and out again?

Killing no one does not work. The speedrunners' way: a ladder over the wall onto the tower, and down through the hole in its roof to the bell fragments; then the tower is dug under from four sides until a chest falls out on one of them, with more loot, while the mobs falling in are killed. Or a battering ram: the outer wall first, then the tower. Or a catapult: it throws whoever stands in it, so it can send you over the wall instead of the ram or the ladder. The fortress also gives the molten cores of the stone portal, the one portal that carries metal, from the stands inside it.

## How do I find Fader, fast?

His Vegvísir stands in the Charred Fortresses and the Charred Ruins. With the mobs behind you, the shorter the way the better: a Fader near the north–south line through the start is a shorter sail from the copper base. Killing him is the lesser question: the weapon, the buff, a pit dug under him, a wall round his arena against the other mobs ([How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#fader)). His ribs build the Eternal Pyre, whose embers the Deep North asks for.

> **Speedrunners** climb a ladder over the wall onto the tower, drop through the hole in its roof to the fragments, and dig under the tower for its chest.

> **With no portals**, this is the hardest step: a full base on the last island north of the Ashlands, then a hut on a sea spire, bridges from spire to spire and a stone walkway to the shore; a cleared fortress becomes the base, and trees will not grow there. Bring a spare Drakkar's materials.

> **On a reverse run** the Ashlands come before the Drakkar: players bridge or swim between the sea's spires, or cross with the grappling hook. Without Moder they give food and Asksvin eggs, but no flametal.

## Can you skip it?

At a price the Deep North collects: without Fader there are no embers, so no Snow Shovel, and without flametal no Flametal set to wear into the cold.
