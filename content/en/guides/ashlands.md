# Ashlands

**What do I need from the Ashlands?** Fader's bells, then his embers: sail the Drakkar over the burning sea and clear the Charred Fortresses near his altar for the fragments.

The Queen is dead and the sea ahead is on fire. The Ashlands spawn creatures in great numbers, and the Charred keep raiding your bases until Fader dies ([World events](/catalog/events)). Before you land, take off the feather cape you brought from the [Mistlands](/guides/mistlands): it doubles the fire you take, unless a fire resistance mead goes over it ([Meads](/catalog/meads)).

## Which boat crosses the sea?

The Ashlands' water burns every boat but the Drakkar, and the longer a boat sails, the worse it fares; the Drakkar alone lands you whole ([Ships and carts](/catalog/ships)).

## Do I need the Drakkar?

Yes, if you mean to come and go: you will land, fight, settle and sail back for more, and the Drakkar is the one boat that survives every crossing. Until a stone portal stands on this shore, it is also the one way your flametal reaches home, since no metal passes a wooden portal.

## Where do I build, and in what?

Ash and lava wear away every piece with no roof over it, so a foothold in the Ashlands is roofed from the first wall. Build it in grausten, the Ashlands' own stone: it holds more than any other building stone, and a straight tower of it stands {{buildHeightGrausten}} m ([Stone building](/catalog/stone-building)); every material's height is in [How high can a wall or a lookout stand?](/guides/boss-arenas#how-high-can-a-wall-or-a-lookout-stand).

## How do I get in, and out again?

There is no way in without dealing with the walls, and the archers and ballistas on them. Break the outer wall with a battering ram, then the tower; or stand in a catapult, which launches whoever stands in it. Both take [flametal](/catalog/materials#flametalnew), smelted at the blast furnace from the ore in the Dvergr's crates in the Charred tower ruins.

## How do I build Fader's arena?

Ring his altar with roofed stations, fires and torches before you summon him: each keeps the open world's spawns and the raids out of its reach, lit or not, and a ring that covers a Morgen hole keeps its Morgen asleep. A Charred spawner is the exception: it spawns inside any base, so set the ring away from one or break it first; a plain one makes only unstarred Charred. Roof everything you raise, or the ash wears it away ([Boss arenas](/guides/boss-arenas)).

## What do I take north?

Fader, above all: the Ashlands cannot be skipped on the way to the Deep North. His ribs build the Eternal Pyre at a stonecutter, its embers and seal pelts make the Ember Charges ({{emberChargePelts}} pelts and {{emberChargeEmbers}} ember make {{emberCharges}}), and only those charges break the petrified trolls, whose tissue is the north's Bloodgold. Take flametal too: the Black Forge at level {{keyForgeLevel}}, which the north's key asks, takes {{keyForgeFlametal}} by its easiest extensions ([How much metal a run takes](/guides/how-much-metal)). His power halves the fire you take and the stagger ([Trophies](/catalog/trophies)).

## How do I land in the Ashlands with no portal?

This is the hardest step of a game without portals, and it starts on the last island north of the Ashlands: a full base there, fed by the Plains and the Mistlands. From it you cross in the Drakkar, or you bridge the sea with a stone walkway to the shore. On land, raise a walled yard of earth first, then take a cleared fortress as your base. Trees do not grow in the Ashlands, so carry the wood, and carry a spare Drakkar's materials too: with no portal the sea is your only way home, and only the Drakkar crosses it whole. The stone portal is no way out for you: everything comes in by sea or over your bridge.
