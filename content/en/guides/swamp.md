# Swamp

**How do I explore the Swamp?** Camp on dry ground, arm yourself with iron from the nearest Sunken Crypt, then comb the Swamp for its other crypts, its roots and Bonemass's altar.

## How do I reach the Swamp?

The Elder's key is in your pack, and the nearest crypt it opens is far from home:

| From home | As a rule | Middle half of seeds |
|---|---|---|
| The nearest Swamp, m | {{homeSwampM}} | {{homeSwampMLow}}–{{homeSwampMHigh}} |
| The nearest Sunken Crypt, m | {{homeCryptM}} | {{homeCryptMLow}}–{{homeCryptMHigh}} |
| Bonemass's altar, m | {{homeBonemassM}} | {{homeBonemassMLow}}–{{homeBonemassMHigh}} |

At {{homeSwampM}} m this is the first land that is not a walk out and back: you go, you camp, you stay. Whether you go on foot depends on your seed. A Swamp lies on your home's own land on {{islandSwamp}}% of seeds, and a crypt in it on {{swampOnFoot}}%, and then the first iron is carried home on foot. On the other seeds you sail the Karve along the coast until a Swamp shows.

## Where do I camp in the Swamp?

On dry ground, and there are two kinds of it.

The first is the top of a Sunken Crypt. The crypt's roof and the patch round it are dry ground inside the Swamp itself, and a camp there puts the portal, the workbench and even a smelter a few steps from the iron. That matters when the crypt stands deep: the crypt nearest the shore lies {{cryptShoreM}} m from it as a rule ({{cryptShoreMLow}}–{{cryptShoreMHigh}} m on the middle half of seeds), and every load is carried that far, on your back or on a cart, before the boat takes it.

The second is the next land along the shore, Meadows or Black Forest, never the Plains, which kill a newcomer at once. A camp there is safe to sleep in, and you creep into the Swamp for the first iron and out again. It costs the longer carry.

The crypt's roof costs you safety and the outside camp costs you carrying; the iron is the same.

## What kills a newcomer in the Swamp?

The water, first. You are wet from the moment you step in, your stamina comes back slowly, and the leeches live where it is deepest: hoe a path or lay planks, and never fight standing in it.

Everything here poisons, the blobs and oozers most of all, and the poison resistance mead works only if you drink it before the blow, not after. The blobs burst under a mace, a bronze one until the iron one is made. The Draugr rise from their body piles for as long as a pile stands, so break the pile before you fight what came out of it. Wraiths come at night, so work by day. The surtlings at the fire holes burn you, and drop coal and cores.

The Abomination rises out of the ground itself. It takes almost nothing from an arrow or a spear, and fire hurts it: fight it with an axe and a torch, on dry ground, and it leaves you its roots.

## How do I get the iron?

You dig it. The Elder's key opens a Sunken Crypt's door, and inside, the muck piles that block the passages are the ore: the antler pickaxe breaks them into iron scrap. The chests add to it now and then: {{cryptChestIron}}% of them hold scrap, {{cryptIronMin}} to {{cryptIronMax}} at a time, and {{cryptChestBone}}% hold one of the withered bones you will need at the end.

No metal passes a portal. Either the scrap goes home, by cart to the boat and by boat to your smelter, or you smelt it where you dug it, in a smelter by the crypt fed with a fire hole's coal, and walk the finished gear through the portal.

The first bars go into the iron mace and the iron pickaxe: the mace because nearly everything here, Bonemass included, breaks under blunt, the pickaxe because nothing less breaks the Mountain's silver. The armour and the banded shield come next, and in them you can stay in the Swamp as long as you like.

More than you expect, if you raise what you make:

| The iron armour, helmet, chest and legs, as made | {{ironSetQ1}} |

| The banded shield, at its top | {{bandedShieldTop}} |

Bonemass's own Swamp holds about {{swampIron}} iron in its muck piles as a rule, and the whole kit's {{kitIronIron}} on only {{swampIronAgeKit}}% of seeds. So one Swamp gives the armour as made and a raised mace with iron to spare; the whole kit at its top takes a second Swamp's crypts.

## How much iron will the later lands ask for?

Iron is the one metal the game sends you back for:

| What it builds, lands later | Iron |
|---|---|
| The blast furnace | {{furnaceIron}} |
| The Drakkar's {{drakkarNails}} iron nails | {{drakkarIron}} |
| The Black Forge's extensions, without the Vice's mechanical springs | {{keyForgeIron}} |
| The frost foundry of the Deep North | {{foundryIron}} |

All four are built lands later and paid for here. Each is a trip back to a Swamp unless its iron already lies in your chest, so keep a portal by a crypt you have not emptied.

## What does the whole Swamp hold?

In iron armour you comb the whole Swamp. Bonemass's own holds:

| In Bonemass's own Swamp | As a rule | Middle half of seeds |
|---|---|---|
| Sunken Crypts | {{swampCrypts}} | {{swampCryptsLow}}–{{swampCryptsHigh}} |
| Surtlings' fire holes | {{swampHoles}} | {{swampHolesLow}}–{{swampHolesHigh}} |
| Iron scrap in the crypts' muck piles, about | {{swampIron}} | {{swampIronLow}}–{{swampIronHigh}} |

Open every crypt: the iron is there, the withered bones are there, and {{vegvisirCrypt}}% of the crypts hold Bonemass's Vegvísir, as the Swamp's ruins do. The [seed map](/tools/map) shows the crypts and the fire holes of your own seed.

The Swamp also gives what no other land does, and you will want all of it: turnip seeds for the garden, thistle, the leeches' bloodbags, guck from high on the trees, and the ancient bark of its old trees, which with iron nails builds the Longship. A little iron makes the stonecutter, and stone becomes a thing to build with. Sausages and turnip stew are the Swamp's food. Before you leave, brew the frost resistance mead, which takes {{frostMeadBloodbags}} bloodbags a base: the Mountain is next, and it freezes.

## How do I get the roots for the Root Harnesk?

From {{harnessTwo}} Abominations. The Root Harnesk halves pierce damage, and pierce is what the Deathsquito of the Plains kills with, and what most of the bosses ahead strike with too.

An Abomination rises from deep Swamp ground the first time you walk onto it, so you find them by sweeping ground you have not walked, not by waiting where you stand. One sweep of Bonemass's own Swamp raises {{swampAbominations}} as a rule: the {{harnessTwo}} of the harness on {{swampHarness}}% of seeds, the {{harnessFour}} of the harness and the leggings on {{swampHarnessLegs}}%. Where the second never rises, a second Swamp gives it quicker than waiting does; one lies within {{secondSwampReachM}} m of his altar on {{secondSwampNear}}% of seeds.

## Where is the Bog Witch?

In a Swamp, and nothing leads to her. She stands in Bonemass's own Swamp on {{swampWitch}}% of seeds, so you meet her while you comb it, or you do not. What she sells is on [What to buy from the traders](/catalog/traders).

## Where is Bonemass's altar?

Where his Vegvísir shows it, in the deep of the Swamp. The altar takes {{witheredBones}} withered bones.

His ground is the choice. {{altarWaterBonemass}}% of the ground within {{arenaRadius}} m of his altar lies under water as a rule, and nothing can be built on water. So either you prepare it, the ground raised with the hoe and a wall round it so nothing wanders in ([Boss arenas](/guides/boss-arenas) has the rest), or you leave it as it is and fight him among the trees. Preparing costs time; not preparing costs you the Draugr and the blobs that join in.

He is weak to blunt and to frost; blades do less to him, and arrows and fire almost nothing: the iron mace, raised as high as your iron allows, and the poison resistance mead drunk before you light the altar. Every way to kill him is on [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#bonemass).

His death gives you the wishbone, which leads you to silver and to buried treasure, as the raven says; his power, {{bonemassPowerPercent}}% less blunt, slash and pierce damage for {{powerMinutes}} minutes; and an end to the Draugr's raids on your home. It also opens raids that never end, ghosts first, then surtlings and bats once you have killed one of each: build for them.

You leave the Swamp in iron, with a mace, an iron pickaxe, a stonecutter, a Longship if you took the bark, turnips at home and frost resistance mead in the chest. The wishbone finds silver, and silver lies where the cold kills an unprepared Viking: the [Mountain](/guides/mountain). On the clock the Swamp is one crypt, a mace and the boss in one trip, and the [Speedrun](/guides/speedrun#swamp) tells that.
