# Swamp

**What do I need from the Swamp?** Iron from the Sunken Crypts and Bonemass's wishbone, which finds silver: sail to a big Swamp, open its crypts with the Elder's key, find his altar.

## How do I reach the Swamp?

Bronze on your back, you go for iron next: on foot for the first of it, as often as not; by boat for the boss. Your start island, the land of [A good start](/guides/a-good-start#what-is-on-my-starting-island), often hides a Swamp of its own, mostly with a crypt in it: iron without a voyage.

## How do I get the iron?

Dig for it. The iron lies in the crypts' muck piles, and your antler pickaxe breaks them; the chests throw in a stack now and then, and the withered bones his altar wants.

|---|---|
| Iron scrap, {{cryptIronMin}} to {{cryptIronMax}} | {{cryptChestIron}}% of chests |

Your first bars most likely become the [iron mace](/catalog/weapons) for Bonemass. They are far from your last: the rest of the game sends you back to iron again and again, for a pickaxe, for stations, for a ship and for the key of the far north.

Leave the Swamp short and you sail back for the rest. [How much metal a run takes](/guides/how-much-metal) adds up every age.

## Is there enough iron in Bonemass's Swamp for everything?

Seldom, if you want it all. A home that stays makes the whole iron age, the armour, the shield, a weapon of every kind and the tools, each raised to its top, and that bill runs to hundreds. Most of it lies in the muck piles: the chests alone would take about {{cryptChestsIronKit}} of them to pay for every iron piece of the game, {{kitAllIron}} iron.

## Where do I smelt it, and how does it get home?

At home, by the way you built in. Iron never passes a portal, so every scrap of it rides back to your home's smelter by cart and boat. The Swamp is where players first get lost: lay wooden walkways over the water from the shore to the crypt nearest it, or a raised causeway between the trees, and hoe a road on the dry ground for the cart, as players do; then roll the iron to the shore and sail it home.

## How do I get the roots for the Root Harnesk?

From {{harnessTwo}} Abominations. The Root Harnesk halves pierce, which the Deathsquito deals on the Plains and most of the bosses deal too. An Abomination rises from the deep Swamp as you first walk into it, and more in its bog patches, so sweep fresh ground round his altar rather than wait where you stand.

## Should I sweep a second Swamp for the Root Harnesk?

On a small Swamp, yes. A big one gives both Abominations on one sweep; a small one gives the first and keeps you waiting long for the next. When the second does not come, the faster harness is in another Swamp's bog patches.

## Where is Bonemass's altar?

Follow his Vegvísir: it stands in the Swamp's ruins and inside {{vegvisirCrypt}}% of the Sunken Crypts, and his altar takes {{witheredBones}} withered bones, from the crypts' chests. Blunt damage hurts him most, so bring the iron mace ([How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#bonemass)). His wishbone, worn, beeps like a metal detector near buried silver, and near the Swamp's buried iron if you are short.

Think before you kill him: his death opens [raids](/catalog/events) that never end, ghosts, and surtlings and bats once you have killed one of each, so a home built in this age stays under them for good.

## How do I wall in Bonemass's arena?

With the base's own ground. A ring of stations or fires keeps the open world and every placed spawner from raising anything inside it, no Draugr, leech, blob or Abomination: {{ringPiecesCampfire}} campfires or {{ringPiecesWorkbench}} workbenches ring {{arenaRadius}} m round his altar, and [Boss arenas](/guides/boss-arenas#which-pieces-make-the-ground-a-base) has the rest of the pieces. A bone pile is the one thing a ring does not stop, and a fire hole's surtlings come back minutes after the last dies, so look at what stands near before you build.

## Where is the Bog Witch?

Now and then by Bonemass, on a big Swamp: she stands in his own Swamp on {{swampWitch}}% of seeds, and then as a rule {{bonemassWitchM}} m from his altar ({{bonemassWitchMLow}}–{{bonemassWitchMHigh}} m). Players come to her for the squirrel hamstrings of the Tonic of Ratatosk and the cooking she sells to those who stay: [What to buy from the traders](/catalog/traders).

## How do I race through the Swamp?

For the speedrunner, who wants the mace, the pickaxe and the boss in one trip, then out. Stop digging once the chests and piles give the mace's iron and the pickaxe's, and count on a second visit for the stations: one more crypt then is quicker than a long dig now. A run to Kall spends {{runIron}} iron in all, the mace and the pickaxe included, as the [Speedrun](/guides/speedrun#how-much-metal-does-a-run-spend) guide counts it; the least a run to Kall needs, its gates alone, is {{leastIronDeepNorth}}.

Smelt where the ore lies. A smelter and a forge by the crypt, fed by a surtlings' fire hole's coal and cores, turn the scrap into the mace on the spot, and best of all when the crypt by the fire hole is also the one by his altar, so the iron and the boss are one trip.

With no fire hole near, it is the bad case: the scrap rides by cart and boat to the copper base's smelter. His Swamp seldom spares you that sail: it lies on the copper base's own land, no sea between, on only {{swampOnBaseLand}}% of seeds.

Bonemass himself goes down quicker under a shelf: build a roof, get under it and hit him from below. And if you will not leave for a second Swamp, skip the harness on a small one: on the Plains a bow in hand kills a Deathsquito with one arrow.

## Coming from the north without the Elder's key: where is the iron?

For the [reverse run](/guides/reverse-run) that has left the Elder alive: without his key a crypt's door stays shut, and its chests and piles with it. The north asks for iron all the same, {{firstKeyIron}} for its own stations alone, the blast furnace, the frost foundry, the Black Forge's extensions with the Vice's mechanical springs, and the stonecutter, more than a mace. It comes from what you break on the way ([What to break](/catalog/breakables)).

| Breaking for iron, outside the crypts | One break gives iron |
|---|---|
| A frost cave's wooden gate, an iron bar | {{ironFrostGate}}% of breaks |
| An Ashlands pot, an iron bar | {{ironAshPot}}% of breaks |
| The Winding Tunnels' rubble, iron scrap | {{ironTunnelRubble}}% of breaks |
| The Winding Tunnels' wardrobe shafts, iron scrap | {{ironTunnelShaft}}% of breaks |
| A Mörkhalla's weapon stand, iron scrap | {{ironMorkStand}}% of breaks |

The Winding Tunnels come before the Mörkhalla anyway, for the key's mould, so clear their rubble as you go. What the Swamp still gives you is its leeches: every leech drops a bloodbag, and a [frost resistance mead](/catalog/meads#meadfrostresist)'s base takes {{frostMeadBloodbags}} of them.

## Can you skip the Swamp?

At a price: without the wishbone, silver means digging the Mountain blind and hoping Moder's shots dig it out, a poor hope; without iron the forge stays where bronze left it, and the iron pickaxe never comes.
