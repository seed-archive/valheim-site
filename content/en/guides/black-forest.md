# Black Forest

**What do I need from the Black Forest?** Copper and tin for bronze, best from a troll cave by the shore, and the Elder's swamp key, which opens the Sunken Crypts.

## Getting there

The start sits in a ring of Meadows: the nearest Black Forest is usually {{forestMedian}} m away, and rarely nearer than {{forestLeast}} m. On {{forestIsland}}% of seeds the start island has Black Forest of its own. Greydwarfs throw stones from the first trees; the [Meadows](/guides/meadows) chapter ends with the pickaxe you need here.

## Copper, tin and bronze

Settle at a troll cave: the ledge at its mouth is flat ground, which the Black Forest rarely gives, and {{caveVeins}} copper veins or more by the entrance are the sign to stop there. Of the {{caves}} troll caves nearest the start, usually {{copperCaves}} have that much copper by the entrance; the nearest of them is usually {{copperNearest}} m from the start, and the whole way of the first days, from the start by Eikthyr's altar to that cave, is usually {{walk}} m.

Tin grows only at the water's edge. Within {{reach}} m of that cave there are usually {{tin}} tin deposits, and the cave is usually {{shore}} m from the sea: copper and tin in one trip, and the boat at the door. A bar of [bronze](/catalog/materials#bronze) is {{bronzeCopper}} copper and {{bronzeTin}} tin at the forge; what to make of it is on the [weapons](/catalog/weapons), [armour](/catalog/armour) and [tools](/catalog/tools) pages, and the [craft tree](/tools/craft-tree) totals the ore.

## Wood without an axe

A troll fells trees, the birches and oaks of fine wood included, and mines copper, tin and even silver: lure it past them. The Elder's blows fell the plain trees round his altar, not the birches and oaks. Between them they cut the wood a run needs, so the axe can wait. Once the troll has mined the copper, lead it to the birches for the portal's fine wood: within {{baseBirchReach}} m of the copper cave there are usually {{birchGroup}}.

## The Elder

His altar takes {{ancientSeeds}} ancient seeds. Greydwarf nests drop them, and they stand in the Greydwarf camps of the Black Forest; a Greydwarf brute drops one now and then. On half of all seeds the altar is within {{elderMedian}} m of the start, on nine seeds in ten within {{elderMost}} m, and on {{elderIsland}}% of seeds on the start island. He is weak to fire: fire arrows from behind the altar's pillars, or a ring of campfires he walks through, both in [How to kill each boss](/guides/bosses#the-elder).

He drops the swamp key, which opens the door of a Sunken Crypt, and his trophy on its stone at the start makes you gather faster: see [Trophies](/catalog/trophies).

## Haldor

Haldor the trader lives in the Black Forest; his camp is usually {{haldorMedian}} m from the start, and on {{haldorIsland}}% of seeds on the start island. What is worth his coins is in [What to buy from the traders](/catalog/traders).

## Can you skip the Elder?

Yes, as everything in the game can be skipped: the [reverse run](/guides/reverse-run) does. The cost is time and nerve: without his key the door of a Sunken Crypt stays closed to you.

Every troll cave, copper vein, tin deposit and birch of your seed is on the [seed map](/tools/map). Next, iron: the [Swamp](/guides/swamp).
