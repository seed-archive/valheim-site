# Black Forest

**How do I get through the Black Forest fast?** Settle at a troll cave with copper by it and tin on the shore below: the troll mines and fells for you, the Burial Chambers round it give the cores, and the Elder, once his Vegvísir is found, is a short run away.

## Where do I set my base?

By a troll cave with {{caveVeins}} copper veins by it on the shore, and tin there, since bronze is copper and tin. What matters is a troll by the cave; whether one waits inside does not. The ledge at its mouth is flat ground, and the boat is built on the water facing it when the run must sail.

## How do I get copper and tin fast?

The troll mines the copper: lure it onto the veins. The tin at the water's edge you most likely dig yourself. A bar of [bronze](/catalog/materials#bronze) is {{bronzeCopper}} copper and {{bronzeTin}} tin at the forge.

## Where do I get the surtling cores?

From the Burial Chambers, which stand only in the Black Forest: run round the base to find them, and rob the stone towers, the bear's den and whatever else on the way, carrying it home with the berries for food. A run takes {{coresNeed}}: five for the charcoal kiln, five for the smelter, two and two for a pair of portals.

## How do I find the Elder?

Killing him is not the question; finding him is. His Vegvísir stands in the Black Forest's ruins and stone towers, and in {{vegvisirChamber}}% of Burial Chambers, so climb the towers; once you know where he is, run there and kill him (fire arrows, campfires, both in [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#the-elder)). He fells wood too, so set a portal by his altar and send his wood home through it.

Felled by the troll, a bronze axe, or the axe heads found in chests, best near the base or the altars. Once the troll has mined the copper, lead it to the birches.

> **Speedrunners** let the troll mine the copper and fell the fine wood, burn the Elder with fire arrows or campfires, and leave a portal by his altar to send his felled wood home.

> **With no map**, Haldor is found by luck: players circle the start in widening rings, and search every Black Forest instead of sailing past it.

> **With no portals**, set the base where many biomes meet, and carry a kit to set up anywhere: surtling cores, iron for a stonecutter, copper for a forge.

## Can you skip the Elder?

At a cost: without his key the door of a Sunken Crypt stays closed. The [reverse run](/guides/reverse-run) does it anyway.

Every troll cave, copper vein, tin deposit and birch of your seed is on the [seed map](/tools/map). Next, iron: the [Swamp](/guides/swamp).
