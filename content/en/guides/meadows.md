# Meadows

**How do I get through the Meadows fast?** Kill Eikthyr early for his power, which makes the scouting run faster, take his antler for the only pickaxe before bronze, and pick up the beehives, the axes and the coins of the houses you rob on the way.

## Where is Eikthyr's altar?

You look for him for speed: his trophy hung on its stone at the start cuts the stamina that running, jumping and swimming cost, so the scouting run reaches Bonemass and Moder sooner and learns sooner whether the seed is worth running at all. The power is in [Trophies](/catalog/trophies). His Vegvísir stands at the start itself.

## How do I kill two deer?

His altar takes {{deerTrophies}} deer trophies, and the deer come to you on the scouting run anyway. What kills them is a throwing weapon: a spear or a bow hits a deer far more surely than a club chasing it. Stalk them, and throw from cover.

With a spear or a bow Eikthyr himself is a light fight; on a speedrun he is burnt with campfires. Both ways are in [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#eikthyr).

## What do I make from his antler?

The antler pickaxe, almost always: one antler and {{pickaxeWood}} wood at a workbench, and it mines copper and tin until bronze. The [tools](/catalog/tools) page has every pickaxe.

## Where are the beehives?

Not looked for on purpose: you rob the Draugr villages and the abandoned houses on the way, and take the bee out of them with the axe heads that cut fine wood and the coins for the trader. Four hives are usually enough, five are luck. The [meads](/catalog/meads) list what the honey turns into.

## Is Hildir worth looking for?

No one looks for her: traders are seen from far off and met on the way, while the run follows the Vegvísir to the boss as fast as it can.

> **Speedrunners** burn Eikthyr with campfires, make the antler pickaxe and nothing else from his antler, and take the hives only from the houses they rob anyway.

> **With no map**, read a Vegvísir and leave the mouse alone: it turns your camera toward its boss. Yggdrasil's branch in the sky is a compass: it hangs fixed over the world, not over you, running from its root beyond the east-north-east edge to its tip a little south-west of the start.

> **With no portals**, every boss trophy still comes back to the stones at the start: keep a road or a dock for the trip.

## Can you skip Eikthyr?

Not on a run: without his antler there is no pickaxe, and without his power every step of the scouting costs full stamina. A [reverse run](/guides/reverse-run) gets round it with a troll's blows.
