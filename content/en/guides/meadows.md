# Meadows

**What do I need from the Meadows?** Eikthyr's antler, for the only pickaxe before bronze, and the beehives of the abandoned houses and Draugr villages, for every mead of the run.

## Eikthyr

His altar takes {{deerTrophies}} deer trophies: hunt deer on the way to it. What matters is that it stands on the start island, as on {{eikthyrIsland}}% of seeds; on the rest the first boat comes before the first boss. Close is a bonus: on half of all seeds the altar is within {{eikthyrMedian}} m of the start, on nine seeds in ten within {{eikthyrMost}} m. He has no weakness: a bow from behind the big rocks round his altar is the safe way, both ways are in [How to kill each boss](/guides/bosses#eikthyr).

Hang his trophy on its stone at the start: running, jumping and swimming then cost far less stamina, which carries half the game. The power is in [Trophies](/catalog/trophies).

## The antler pickaxe

Eikthyr drops hard antlers. One antler and {{pickaxeWood}} wood at a workbench make the antler pickaxe, the only pickaxe until bronze: it mines copper and tin, and the bronze pickaxe after it mines nothing new. The [tools](/catalog/tools) page has every pickaxe.

## Beehives

Beehives stand in the Meadows' abandoned houses, a house holding one at a {{hiveChance}}% chance, and in the houses and towers of the Draugr villages and farms. A mead base takes {{meadHoney}} honey; a run takes {{hivesFive}} hives home. The [meads](/catalog/meads) list what the honey turns into.

## Fine wood by the start

Fine wood comes only from birches and oaks, and they want an axe of the bronze axe's tier; the Early Axes get there without bronze, from a Curious axe head and a Mysterious axe head found in chests of the abandoned houses. Within {{startWoodM}} m of the start there are usually {{startBirches}} birches.

## How often a seed has it

| What | How often |
|---|---|
| Eikthyr on the start island | {{eikthyrIsland}}% of seeds |
| Hives within a kilometre of the start | {{hivesNear}} as a rule |

Why {{hivesFive}} hives: a hive makes one honey every {{hiveSeconds}} seconds, and a fermenter turns one mead base of {{meadHoneyNeed}} honey every {{fermentSeconds}} seconds. A fermenter that never stands idle takes {{meadHoneyNeed}} × {{hiveSeconds}} ÷ {{fermentSeconds}} = {{hivesFive}} hives, and two take {{hivesTen}}. A speedrun brews far less than that: a handful of meads in the whole game — Ratatosk once, a frost resistance mead for the Mountain, a stamina mead for the Queen — so {{hivesFive}} hives serve two fermenters on a run.

A house holds one hive at most, so hives stand together only in a Draugr village or farm, or where lone houses stand close: head for those first, and gather the rest round the start. [Rate your seed](/tools/how-good-is-my-seed) to see its own.

## Can you skip Eikthyr?

Technically yes, but not on a run: without his antler there is no pickaxe, so no copper, no tin and no bronze, and without his power every run and jump costs full stamina. A [reverse run](/guides/reverse-run) gets round it with a troll's blows.

Every beehive, birch and oak of your seed is on the [seed map](/tools/map). Next, copper and tin: the [Black Forest](/guides/black-forest).
