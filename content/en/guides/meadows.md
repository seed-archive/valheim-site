# Meadows

**How do I explore the Meadows?** Build your home on the shore, dress in boar and deer hide, rob the old houses for bees and axe heads, then bring Eikthyr's antlers home.

## Where do I build my first home?

On open, level ground by the sea. The raven will have you chopping, building and sleeping within the hour, and he does not care where: "wherever home may be", he says. It matters all the same. No portal lets ore or metal through, so every load you will ever smelt comes home in a boat's hold, and a house far from the water means walking each of those loads from the boat to the door, and yourself back to the boat for the next. Build where the boat can lie at your doorstep. From the Sacrificial Stones the open sea is {{startShoreM}} m away as a rule, {{startShoreMLow}}–{{startShoreMHigh}} m on the middle half of seeds: a short walk, made once.

Keep the Black Forest within reach and off your doorstep: on its border the Greydwarfs walk in at night. Keep the hall small, too: the open ground round it is for the gardens, and for the oaks you will plant for fine wood; and [comfort](/catalog/comfort), which decides how long you stay rested, counts only the pieces close round you. The stones themselves give a house nothing; you come back to them to read a Vegvísir and to hang a trophy. How far every later haul lies from such a home, and where a bridge spares the boat, is weighed on [Where to build your base](/guides/where-to-build).

That copper row is the case for the one other sound home: on a Black Forest shore, by a troll's cave. There the ore lies at the door instead of {{homeCopperM}} m away, and so does everything that kills a newcomer. Either way the home goes where the loads end, not where the Valkyrie dropped you.

## What do the Meadows give a newcomer?

You arrive weak, and the Meadows are the one land that lets you live through it: what walks here in the open, the boar, the Neck and the Greyling, falls to a club. So the first gear is made here, before the forest tests it.

Start with your own shore. Flint lies along the water's edge, and with it come the flint axe, the knife and the flint spear. Make the spear even if the bow becomes your weapon: a troll is weak to pierce, and the spear is the first thing you own that hurts one. Boars give the leather scraps of the crude bow and the wood shield; deer give the hides of the leather armour and the cape. The raven tells you to eat; in the Meadows that means boar, deer and Neck tail for health, raspberries and honey for stamina, mushrooms for both. Every dish is on the [food](/catalog/food) page.

Then the old houses. An abandoned house's chest may hold an axe head, the Curious one or the Mysterious, and one of each makes the Early Axes, which fell birch and oak for fine wood long before you smelt any bronze. The Draugr villages are the one real fight of the Meadows: the dead stand in them, and they hit like nothing else here. Come in leather, with a shield.

## Where are the beehives?

In the abandoned houses, one at most in each, and in the Draugr villages and farms, where several rooms may hold one. Break the wild hive, take its queen home and set your own hive by the house. As a rule {{homeHives}} hives stand within {{beesNearM}} m of home, so the question is never whether there are enough.

## How many hives for brewing all game?

As many as one fermenter eats. The honey is for the meads you will brew once the Black Forest gives you bronze, and a fermenter that never stands empty takes the honey of several hives.

| Fermenters kept brewing without a pause | Hives it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 | {{hivesFor1}} |
| 2 | {{hivesFor2}} |
| 3 | {{hivesFor3}} |

How we count it: a mead base takes {{meadHoneyNeed}} honey, a hive makes one honey every {{hiveSeconds}} s, by day and by night, and a fermenter takes {{fermentSeconds}} s over one base. So one fermenter eats {{meadHoneyNeed}} × {{hiveSeconds}} ÷ {{fermentSeconds}} = {{hivesFor1}} hives' honey.

Stop at {{hivesFor1}}. They keep one fermenter busy, and a second fermenter on the same hives only waits a little for its honey; nothing in the game asks for mead faster than that. Take one more if a house on your way holds it, and never walk for the {{hivesFor2}} a second fermenter would want. A hive holds {{hiveHoneyMax}} honey and then stops, so empty it whenever you pass.

## Where is Eikthyr's altar?

His Vegvísir stands among the Sacrificial Stones, and reading it puts the altar on your map.

The altar takes {{deerTrophies}} deer trophies. A deer outruns a club, so stalk it and throw the spear, or shoot from cover. Eikthyr himself strikes with lightning and his antlers, and a wood shield stops every one of his blows. The crude bow with fire arrows hurts him most, then the Early Axes, then the flint axe; the whole fight is on [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#eikthyr). He breaks what he charges, so where his altar stands close to home, lead him away from the house.

## How do I make the antler pickaxe?

From one of his hard antlers and {{pickaxeWood}} wood at the workbench. It is the only pickaxe until bronze, and it breaks the copper and the tin that bronze is made of.

His trophy goes on its stone among the Sacrificial Stones, and from then on his power is yours to call: {{eikthyrPowerPercent}}% less stamina for running, jumping and swimming, for {{powerMinutes}} minutes, ready again {{powerCooldownMinutes}} minutes after you called it. While he lived, his Necks and boars came for your walls now and then; that ends with him.

You leave the Meadows with a home on the shore, leather on your back, a spear or a bow in hand, hives by the door, a pickaxe and a power that makes every walk cheaper. All of it was made where nothing could really hurt you. Not far from your door the trees close in, and under them everything can: the copper that buys your next armour lies at the edge of the [Black Forest](/guides/black-forest). Racing the clock, the Meadows are only a road to that copper, and the [Speedrun](/guides/speedrun#meadows) tells it.
