# Meadows

**What do I do first in the Meadows?** Kill Eikthyr for the antler pickaxe and his stamina power, then raise your first home by the stones and take the bees from the ruins round it.

## Where is Eikthyr's altar?

The saga proper starts where the Valkyrie dropped you, and its first boss lives close: as a rule on your own island, so your first walk out reaches him without a boat; what else the start island holds is in [A good start](/guides/a-good-start). His Vegvísir stands at the start itself. Bring his trophy back and hang it on its stone there: for a while after you call on it, running, jumping and swimming cost far less stamina, and every trip after it comes cheaper. The power is in [Trophies](/catalog/trophies).

> **With no map**, read the Vegvísir at the start and leave the mouse alone: the camera turns toward Eikthyr. Yggdrasil's branch hangs fixed over the world along one line, east-north-east to west-south-west, and [No death, no map, no portals](/guides/other-rules) tells how to steer by it.

## How do I summon and kill Eikthyr?

With {{deerTrophies}} deer trophies on his altar. The deer find you on any walk out; catching them is the trouble. A club chasing a deer comes back empty, while a spear or a bow thrown from cover kills it far more surely, so stalk them.

Eikthyr himself is a light fight with that same spear or bow. Fire burns him too, and every way to kill him is in [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses#eikthyr).

> **With no death**, on a [Very hard](/guides/hardcore) world or not, raise your spear, club and blocking on the training dummy before Eikthyr, and fight him inside a ring of campfires, where no Neck or Boar rises during the fight.

## How do I make the antler pickaxe?

From his antler and {{pickaxeWood}} wood at a workbench. It is the only pickaxe until bronze, and it mines the copper and tin of the Black Forest. Every pickaxe is on the [tools](/catalog/tools) page.

> **With no portals**, his trophy and every one after it is carried back to the stones at the start, so the road from your dock to the stones is the first road you build.

## Where do I build my first home?

By the stones, and in plain wood: the first home goes up from any tree your axe fells. Raise it among the birches and oaks round the start all the same, since of the Meadows' trees only those two give fine wood, and the portal and the Karve will want it. The fine wood waits for an Early Axe, if a chest in the ruins that hold the beehives gives you its axe heads, along with coins for the trader, or for a bronze axe; how many of those trees grow round the start is in [Where do I get fine wood?](/guides/black-forest#where-do-i-get-fine-wood). The home that lasts the whole game is weighed in [Where to build your base](/guides/where-to-build).

> **At leisure**, do not raise your hall long before Eikthyr: his army stays among the raids the game can roll on you until the day he dies.

## Where are the beehives?

In the Meadows' abandoned houses, one hive at most in each, and in the Draugr villages and farms, where several rooms may hold one: a village is where hives stand together. Later the Black Forest adds a few more, one in a stone tower ruin, one in the bear's den, where the bear is likely asleep inside. Take each bee home and set it by your own walls; the [meads](/catalog/meads) show what its honey becomes. As a rule {{hivesNear}} hives stand within {{beesNearM}} m of the start, and nearly every start island holds all you will need.

## How many hives for brewing all game?

One set for every fermenter you mean to keep busy, once the forge is hot: the fermenter and the mead ketill are bronze-age work, a biome away, but the bees you take now are the honey waiting for them. A hive makes a honey every {{hiveSeconds}} s, day and night alike, holds {{hiveHoneyMax}} and stops when full, so empty it within {{hiveHoneyMax}} × {{hiveSeconds}} s or the bees idle. A mead base takes {{meadHoneyNeed}} honey and ferments in {{fermentSeconds}} s, so each fermenter that never stands empty eats its own hives:

## Is Hildir worth looking for?

For her clothes, later: she is seen from far off and met on the way, so few go looking. Her quests send you into her Smouldering Tomb and Howling Cavern, where every guard carries at least one star, so go geared; what she sells is in [What to buy from the traders](/catalog/traders).

## How do I race through the Meadows?

For the speedrunner, to whom the Meadows are only the road to the copper base, a troll cave of the [Black Forest](/guides/black-forest); the whole route is on the [Speedrun](/guides/speedrun) page, along with how often Eikthyr and Hildir lie on the run's [strip](/guides/speedrun#what-is-the-strip). For you Eikthyr's power is a bonus, never a must: it pays when his altar lies near the way to the base cave, since your first run out goes there anyway. The detour is {{eikthyrDetourM}} m as a rule, and {{eikthyrDetourMLow}}–{{eikthyrDetourMHigh}} m on the middle half of seeds; with his altar far off the way, go without his power.

When you do take him, burn him: ring his altar with campfires. They do not last, and they need not, since the burning they set does the work. Their ground counts as a base ([Boss arenas](/guides/boss-arenas#which-pieces-make-the-ground-a-base)), so nothing of the open world rises round them while he burns. Make the antler pickaxe from his antler and nothing else, and run on.

Take the hives only from what you rob on that first walk out, to Eikthyr and on to the base cave, and turn aside for none. {{hivesFive}} hives serve a run, since it brews only a handful of times a game, and {{hivesFive}} stand within {{wayReachM}} m of that walk on {{hivesOnWayFive}}% of seeds.

## Can you skip Eikthyr?

You can, and it costs more than the walk to him. Without his antler nothing mines before bronze, so a troll's blows must do the digging at every vein; without his power every step costs full stamina; and his raid stays possible until the day he dies.

> **On a reverse run** you may leave with only the raft's wood, leather scraps and resin, and the honey for a frost resistance mead, whose base also wants the Swamp's bloodbags. But Eikthyr is as a rule the nearest boss of the world, and his antler pickaxe breaks the Deep North's ice chunks and frozen statues and the tin you meet on the way back: killing him first costs one short walk. The rest is on the [Reverse run](/guides/reverse-run).

[How good is my seed?](/tools/how-good-is-my-seed) sets your own seed against these numbers, and the [seed map](/tools/map) shows where its altar, houses and villages stand.
