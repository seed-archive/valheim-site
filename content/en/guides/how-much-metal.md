# How much metal a run takes

**How much iron do I need?** For a full kit at top quality, {{kitAllIron}} iron and {{kitAllSilver}} silver. The least a run to Kall needs is only {{leastIronDeepNorth}} iron.

## What does each boss's summon take?

Your walls stand by the water, and now the bosses start sending their bills. Every altar wants an offering from its boss's own biome, and up to Yagluth you simply pick it up on the way. The last three you have to make, and that is where your metal starts to bleed.

|---|---|---|
| Eikthyr | {{deerTrophies}} deer trophies | found |
| The Elder | {{ancientSeedsNeed}} ancient seeds | found |
| Bonemass | {{witheredBones}} withered bones, from the Sunken Crypts | found |
| Moder | {{dragonEggs}} dragon eggs, from the drake nests | found |
| Yagluth | {{yagluthTotemsNeed}} Fuling totems | found |
| The Queen | the Sealbreaker, {{sealbreakerFragments}} fragments | the Galdr Table, {{galdrYggdrasil}} Yggdrasil wood, {{galdrBlackMetal}} black metal, {{galdrBlackCores}} black cores, {{galdrEitr}} eitr |
| Fader | {{bells}} bells, {{bellFragments}} fragments each | the Black Forge |
| Kall Fimbulbringer | {{maliciousBlood}} Malicious Blood, one from each Jotun invasion a Mörkhalla's ice sets loose, every Mörkhalla opened with an Intricate Key its gate consumes | {{dnKeys}} Intricate Keys, {{keyBloodgold}} Bloodgold each, cast at the Black Forge, then hardened at the frost foundry |

None of it waits in a friendly place. The bones lie behind the Elder's key in the [Swamp](/guides/swamp), the eggs up on the [Mountain](/guides/mountain), the totems in the villages of the [Plains](/guides/plains), the fragments deep in the Infested Mines of the [Mistlands](/guides/mistlands), the bells' fragments in the Charred Fortresses of the [Ashlands](/guides/ashlands), and the key's mould in the Winding Tunnels of the [Deep North](/guides/deep-north). When you get there, [How to kill each boss](/catalog/bosses) has the fights.

## What do the roads between biomes make you build?

Between the altars stand a ship and a handful of stations the road will not let you skip, and each one eats metal you would rather keep.

| Gate | What it opens | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| The Drakkar | the Ashlands' burning sea, which every other boat burns on | {{drakkarNails}} iron nails ({{drakkarIron}} iron); the rest of it on the [Ashlands](/guides/ashlands#which-boat-crosses-the-sea) |
| The blast furnace | black metal, flametal and Bloodgold | {{furnaceStone}} stone, {{furnaceCores}} surtling cores, {{furnaceIron}} iron, {{furnaceFineWood}} fine wood, at the artisan table, from Moder's tears |
| The Black Forge, level {{keyForgeLevel}} | the bells and the Intricate Key | with its three easiest extensions: {{keyForgeBlackMarble}} black marble, {{keyForgeYggdrasil}} Yggdrasil wood, {{keyForgeBlackCores}} black cores, {{keyForgeIron}} iron, {{keyForgeCopper}} copper, {{keyForgeSprings}} mechanical springs, {{keyForgeFlametal}} flametal, {{keyForgeBlackwood}} ashwood, {{keyForgeCharredBones}} charred bones |
| The frost foundry | the Intricate Key and everything the Deep North forges | {{foundryIron}} iron, {{foundryStone}} stone, {{foundryFrostCores}} frost cores |
| The stonecutter | the sharpening stone of the forge's top level | {{stonecutterIron}} iron |

Every level and extension is on the [crafting stations](/catalog/crafting-stations) page, every boat on [Ships](/catalog/ships).

> **On a reverse run** Moder is your wall: the blast furnace is built at the artisan table, and the table from her tears, so without her there is no Bloodgold, no key and no Kall. Fader is the other: the petrified trolls break only to bombs made with his embers.

## What does a full kit take, age by age?

This is the bill you actually pay: each age's armour set plus one weapon of every kind its station makes, all raised to top quality. That last part is the trap. Each level above the first adds the item's per-level amount once, then twice, then four times, so the last level alone costs as much as the craft and every level before it.

| Age | Bronze | Iron | Silver | Black metal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze, the Black Forest | {{kitBronzeBronze}} | {{kitBronzeIron}} | {{kitBronzeSilver}} | {{kitBronzeBlackMetal}} |
| Iron, the Swamp | {{kitIronBronze}} | {{kitIronIron}} | {{kitIronSilver}} | {{kitIronBlackMetal}} |
| Silver, the Mountain | {{kitSilverBronze}} | {{kitSilverIron}} | {{kitSilverSilver}} | {{kitSilverBlackMetal}} |
| Black metal, the Plains | {{kitBlackMetalBronze}} | {{kitBlackMetalIron}} | {{kitBlackMetalSilver}} | {{kitBlackMetalBlackMetal}} |
| All four | {{kitAllBronze}} | {{kitAllIron}} | {{kitAllSilver}} | {{kitAllBlackMetal}} |

Every piece's steps are on [Weapons](/catalog/weapons) and [Armour](/catalog/armour); what each bar is smelted from, on [Materials](/catalog/materials).

> **At leisure**, raise only the age you will wear longest: the top level of a piece you shed in the next biome is the dearest metal you will ever spend.

> **At leisure**, the kit is the plan, not the ceiling. The [Forge of Potential](/guides/forge-of-potential) can push a piece past its top level, but only at a chance, and shatters it otherwise: bring it only a spare.

## Does my seed hold it?

### Is there iron enough in Bonemass's Swamp, and silver by Moder?

You can know before you sail. The muck piles in the Sunken Crypts of Bonemass's own Swamp hold a fixed amount of iron scrap, and silver sits in veins by Moder's altar, so your seed has settled both questions already.

> **With no portals**, the bill is the haul: every scrap of iron and every bar of silver goes home by ship or cart, so a seed whose Swamp covers the kit is one long trip instead of several.

## Racing a category: the least metal each takes

For the runner on the clock in an All Bosses category of the [Speedrun](/guides/speedrun), the kit is dead weight. Strip the run to the bone, every station built once, weapons and armour your own business, and this is the least metal each category can get away with. A Classic run picks up every summon it needs and makes none.

| Category | Iron | Silver | Black metal | Flametal | Bloodgold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Classic, to Yagluth | {{leastIronClassic}} | {{leastSilverClassic}} | {{leastBlackMetalClassic}} | {{leastFlametalClassic}} | {{leastBloodgoldClassic}} |
| Mistlands, to the Queen | {{leastIronMistlands}} | {{leastSilverMistlands}} | {{leastBlackMetalMistlands}} | {{leastFlametalMistlands}} | {{leastBloodgoldMistlands}} |
| Ashlands, to Fader | {{leastIronAshlands}} | {{leastSilverAshlands}} | {{leastBlackMetalAshlands}} | {{leastFlametalAshlands}} | {{leastBloodgoldAshlands}} |
| Deep North, to Kall | {{leastIronDeepNorth}} | {{leastSilverDeepNorth}} | {{leastBlackMetalDeepNorth}} | {{leastFlametalDeepNorth}} | {{leastBloodgoldDeepNorth}} |

The pickaxe and the axe that dig each material out are on the bill; nothing you swing at a boss is. Real runs overspend on purpose: an [iron mace](/catalog/weapons) for Bonemass ({{ironMace}} iron) and the iron pickaxe for silver. Copper and tin they leave to a troll, whose blows mine them, so a run that leads one along saves the pickaxe.

You know the bill, and every boss that bill buys will try to kill you for it. Last of the basics, how to claim the ground round an altar so the fight is yours alone: [Boss arenas](/guides/boss-arenas).
