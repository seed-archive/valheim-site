# A good start

**What makes a good start?** A start island that spares the boat on the way to iron and silver, and a first run out that finds where they will come from before you settle.

## What is on my start island?

Copper is most likely on the start island already; iron and silver are the metals that cost a voyage. Every biome on the island is a voyage not sailed, there and above all back with the load.

## How far can I go without a boat?

The island decides where the run's bases stand. The Black Forest's base, by a troll cave, is on it almost always; a Swamp on it means its iron is carried on foot to the copper; a Mountain on it, the silver.

## Where do I go first?

To Eikthyr first: his power makes every run after it cheaper. Then to the Elder, and only then to Bonemass and Moder. All the way you scout: the run must learn where the iron and the silver will come from, Bonemass's Swamp and Moder's Mountain. Pick up the surtling cores for a portal on the way, mark the copper veins, and rob whatever can be robbed, so there are coins for the trader. A seed with nothing near is dropped here, before it has cost an evening.
