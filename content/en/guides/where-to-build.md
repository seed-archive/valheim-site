# Where to build your base

**Where do I build the home I keep all game?** Where the boat can leave: on open sea, by the trader and the honey, a short haul from iron and silver.

You are back from your first walk out of [A good start](/guides/a-good-start), and you know roughly where the metals lie. Now pick the spot every bar of them gets dragged to. Choose badly and every trip pays a toll for the rest of the game; choose well and the ore boat beaches at your front door. Set your seed against the numbers below with [How good is my seed?](/tools/how-good-is-my-seed) and the [seed map](/tools/map) before the first wall goes up.

## The home you keep all game

### Can my ships reach the open sea?

On most seeds your iron and silver ride home in a hull, so the water by your door has to lead to the Ocean. A lake looks lovely from the porch and keeps your boat prisoner for good, so check the water before the first beam goes down.

The portal and the Karve eat fine wood, and only birches and oaks give it: a home among them keeps that haul to a stroll. How many grow round the start is in [Where do I get fine wood?](/guides/black-forest#where-do-i-get-fine-wood).

### Will raids come to my home?

They will, and you invite them. Set a fire, a workbench and a bed close together and the game counts the place as a base; most [Raids](/catalog/events) come only to a base. The bargain runs both ways: inside its pieces' reach nothing of the open world spawns, though a nest still does. And the hall's [Comfort](/catalog/comfort) decides how long you stay rested. [Boss arenas](/guides/boss-arenas#which-pieces-make-the-ground-a-base) gives each piece's reach.

> **With no death**, raise the home inside its own base area and break the nests near it: the open world spawns nothing inside a base, but a nest spawns anywhere.

## With no portals

For the player who has given the portals up: the home by the stones is only where the road begins.

### How do I move on without portals?

You leave home for good. One base and a bed a biome, each picked for its neighbours, always further out. Players who run this way seldom turn back, except to carry each boss's trophy to the stones. Pack a kit that makes a base anywhere: surtling cores, iron for a stonecutter ({{stonecutterIron}} bars), copper for a forge. From now on everything rides a ship or a cart: the wood, the stone and the food as well as the metal, which never passes a wooden portal anyway ([No death, no map, no portals](/guides/other-rules#how-do-i-play-with-no-portals)).

### Where does the cart road meet the boat?

At the shore nearest each altar and each ore. Every step from the boat is a step you haul: a short walk is a short haul, a long one is a cart road you have to build first. On the Mountain, players smelt the silver right at Moder's altar and carry the bars down.

> **With no map**, set every base on the shore you landed at, because from the sea you find it again. Players mark each landfall with a signpost or a tall beacon, so the way home stays in sight.

### Where do biomes meet?

The best no-portals base stands where the biomes it needs touch: fewer trips, less to fetch. Look for the Swamp's iron beside a Mountain's silver, or the edge where the Plains meet the [Mistlands](/guides/mistlands), for crops and sap at one door; the [Plains](/guides/plains#where-do-the-plains-meet-the-mistlands) guide says how often they touch.

It is a rare find: Bonemass's Swamp borders a Mountain on only {{meetSwampMountain}}% of seeds; as a rule the nearest Mountain lies {{meetAltarMMountain}} m from his altar ({{meetAltarMMountainLow}}–{{meetAltarMMountainHigh}} m on the middle half of seeds).

The road ends on the boiling sea, and landing there with no portal is the hardest step of all: the [Ashlands](/guides/ashlands#how-do-i-land-in-the-ashlands-with-no-portal) guide walks you through it.

The walls are up and the routes are drawn. Before the first voyage for ore, count what the whole road will cost you in bars: [How much metal a run takes](/guides/how-much-metal).
