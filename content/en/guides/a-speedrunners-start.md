# A speedrunner's start

**Is this seed worth the run?** When Eikthyr and the base cave share the starting island, the Elder is near the base, and Bonemass and Moder are a short sail off; reset when a leg is long.

## What is on my starting island?

A run is won or lost on the sea. Every voyage is lost time, and the way back with a hold full of ore costs most, since no metal passes a portal. So the first thing you weigh in a seed is how much of the early game it lets you walk.

Your starting island is everything your feet reach from the Sacrificial Stones, with a swim of up to {{swimM}} m on a full belly of Meadows food, the most your stamina carries you before the water takes you under: a river you swim across, the sea you do not. Every biome on it is a voyage you never sail, and every boss whose altar stands on it dies without a boat.

| Biome | On the starting island | Its boss's altar on it |
|---|---|---|
| Meadows | always | {{islandEikthyr}}% of seeds |
| Black Forest | {{islandBlackForest}}% of seeds | {{islandElder}}% of seeds |
| Swamp | {{islandSwamp}}% of seeds | {{islandBonemass}}% of seeds |
| Mountain | {{islandMountain}}% of seeds | {{islandModer}}% of seeds |
| Plains | {{islandPlains}}% of seeds | {{islandYagluth}}% of seeds |
| Mistlands | {{islandMistlands}}% of seeds | never |
| Ashlands | never | never |
| Deep North | never | never |

Read the second column against the third: a biome on your island is common, its boss on your island much less so. The Mountain is there on {{islandMountain}}% of seeds and Moder's altar on {{islandModer}}%. The late biomes are out of reach by the game's own rule: the Mistlands begin far from the world's centre, where the stones stand, and only brush a lucky island's far edge; the Ashlands and the Deep North begin farther still.

## How far can I go without a boat?

As far as the island's iron goes. Copper is no question: a troll cave with copper and tin by it stands on almost every starting island, and the island's Burial Chambers all but always hold the {{coresNeed}} surtling cores of a kiln, a smelter and a pair of portals. Iron is the question: a Sunken Crypt stands on the island on {{swampOnFoot}}% of seeds, and there the mace's iron costs no voyage. Moder's altar shares the island on {{islandModer}}%. Those two shares are the island's worth: each is a sail the run does not make.

## Where do I scout first?

To Eikthyr, then the Elder, and only then Bonemass and Moder. The first thing a run does is scout: you run until you know where your iron lies and where Moder stands, and you do not settle before you know the route. On the way you pocket the surtling cores, mark the copper veins and rob whatever pays in coins for the trader.

| From the start | As a rule | Middle half of seeds |
|---|---|---|
| Eikthyr's altar, m | {{eikthyrMedian}} | {{eikthyrMedianLow}}–{{eikthyrMedianHigh}} |
| The Elder's altar, m | {{elderMedian}} | {{elderMedianLow}}–{{elderMedianHigh}} |
| Bonemass's altar, m | {{bonemassM}} | {{bonemassMLow}}–{{bonemassMHigh}} |
| Moder's altar, m | {{moderM}} | {{moderMLow}}–{{moderMHigh}} |

Eikthyr is the nearest by far, {{eikthyrMedian}} m as a rule, which is why his power, cheaper running, is taken first: it is in hand for the long legs. Bonemass and Moder are the far ones, {{bonemassM}} m and {{moderM}} m, and they decide the seed. These are straight lines, as the raven flies.

## Where does the metal lie?

Not at the altars but near them. From the start, to the nearest of each:

| From the start, to the nearest | As a rule | Middle half of seeds |
|---|---|---|
| The base cave: a troll cave with copper and tin, m | {{baseM}} | {{baseMLow}}–{{baseMHigh}} |
| Sunken Crypt, for iron, m | {{cryptFromStartM}} | {{cryptFromStartMLow}}–{{cryptFromStartMHigh}} |
| Fuling village: barley, flax, black metal, m | {{villageFromStartM}} | {{villageFromStartMLow}}–{{villageFromStartMHigh}} |
| Silver vein by Moder's altar, m | {{silverFromStartM}} | {{silverFromStartMLow}}–{{silverFromStartMHigh}} |

The base cave, {{baseM}} m off as a rule, is where you will live: the base of the whole run. The copper is never the problem. The crypt, {{cryptFromStartM}} m off, and Moder's vein, {{silverFromStartM}} m, are the far rows, and a seed is decided by them. The [seed map](/tools/map) puts all four in front of you.

## Is this seed worth the run?

A seed shows whether it deserves your evening on the first loop, before it has cost you one. The loop runs start, Eikthyr, the base cave, the Elder, Bonemass, Moder, and its legs run from point to point in the order you travel them, not each from the stones.

| The first loop, leg by leg | As a rule | Middle half of seeds |
|---|---|---|
| Start → Eikthyr's altar, m | {{eikthyrMedian}} | {{eikthyrMedianLow}}–{{eikthyrMedianHigh}} |
| Eikthyr's altar → the base cave, m | {{legEikthyrBaseM}} | {{legEikthyrBaseMLow}}–{{legEikthyrBaseMHigh}} |
| The base cave → the Elder's altar, m | {{legBaseElderM}} | {{legBaseElderMLow}}–{{legBaseElderMHigh}} |
| The Elder's altar → Bonemass's altar, m | {{legElderBonemassM}} | {{legElderBonemassMLow}}–{{legElderBonemassMHigh}} |
| Bonemass's altar → Moder's altar, m | {{legBonemassModerM}} | {{legBonemassModerMLow}}–{{legBonemassModerMHigh}} |

The long legs are the last three: {{legBaseElderM}} m from the base to the Elder, {{legElderBonemassM}} m on to Bonemass and {{legBonemassModerM}} m to Moder as a rule. A seed that shortens any of them is worth the evening.

## When do I reset?

When a test fails. Each row below adds one test to the row above, so you watch the seeds fall away one at a time:

| The seed passes | How often |
|---|---|
| Eikthyr's altar and the base cave both on the starting island | {{resetBase}}% of seeds |
| … and the Elder within {{resetElderM}} m of the base | {{resetElder}}% of seeds |
| … and Bonemass within {{resetBonemassM}} m of the start | {{resetBonemass}}% of seeds |
| … and Moder within {{resetModerM}} m of the start | {{resetModer}}% of seeds |

Only {{resetModer}}% of seeds pass all four, so most runs reset before they smelt anything. [How good is my seed?](/tools/how-good-is-my-seed) runs these tests on your own seed, and [Best seeds](/tools/best-seeds) lists the ones that score highest.

From there the route runs boss by boss, on the clock: the [Speedrun](/guides/speedrun).
